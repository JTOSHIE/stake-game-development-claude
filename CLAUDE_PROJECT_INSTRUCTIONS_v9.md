Measurement CONFIRMS a derived answer, it never discovers one; a measurement disagreeing with the specification is a broken measurement until proven otherwise. (o) CLOSE-SEQUENCE GATES CHAIN WITH &&, so a red blocks the push. NEW 2026-08-21: blocking CI watchers are retired in favour of on-demand polls that abort loudly on empty probe output, because a watcher that returns nothing is indistinguishable from a watcher that returned green.

5. PLATFORM REALITY (mined first-hand from stake-engine.com approval docs; mirrored under docs/stake-engine-live/)
REVIEW AND PUBLICATION. Three anonymous reviewers score independently on a fractional scale, 0 to 3 in steps of about 0.33. THE THREE SCORES SUM: the result is a point total out of 9 and the bar is SIX of NINE, a 2-star average. Below it the game is not published, the thread locks for 3 DAYS, and resubmission is then allowed and is graded fresh (corrected 2026-08-21 by R080 against the live approval page, superseding the previous 'average below 1.0' and '7 days', which were a lower bar and a longer lock; attempt 1 scored 4.3 of 9 against the 6-point threshold and was not published). The 2-star tier is quoted verbatim from docs/stake-engine-live/2026-08-11/approval_guidelines_game_quality_rankings.md line 18, and the third sentence matters as much as the first: "Given to games that show considerable creativity or originality. While they may lack polish compared to more established studios, they still demonstrate strong development quality and attention to detail." The named causes of a low rating are shallow gameplay (a player loses interest after one or two bets), generic AI-generated assets (standard fonts, plain gradients, emoji icons, border effects), mismatched art styles, poor animation, and missing bonus mechanics.
THROUGHPUT. First-time publishers are limited to ONE active review until their first game goes live, and the limit rises to five concurrent reviews once approved; quoted from docs/stake-engine-live/2026-08-09/submission-checklist.md line 141. Recorded honestly: the 2026-08-20 final audit could not find this sentence anywhere in the 2026-08-20 capture set, so it is corroborated by the 2026-08-09 capture and by the owner's reading, and a fresh logged-in capture is owed.
