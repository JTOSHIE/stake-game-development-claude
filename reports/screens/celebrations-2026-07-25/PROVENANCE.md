## Why the curated route, and what was refused

A wincap round is 1 in 100,000 in base mode, so waiting for one is not a strategy. The
large and wincap tiers are served from CURATED ROUNDS OUT OF THE SHIPPED BOOK and played
through the normal presentation path: real outcomes the maths genuinely produces, not
forced UI states.

Deliberately refused: setting winAmount directly to fake a tier. The dev hook allows it
and it would have been quicker, but the celebration in the evidence would then never have
corresponded to a real round.
