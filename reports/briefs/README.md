# Briefs

Pasted briefs are saved here verbatim, per conventions (b) and (f), and committed with the
work they drive.

Verbatim means verbatim. A brief is the evidence for every claim its session report makes,
so it is never tidied, summarised or corrected on the way in.

Briefs issued before 2026-07-25 are at `reports/archive/briefs/`, where they were moved
when the repository root was reduced to its functional set.
