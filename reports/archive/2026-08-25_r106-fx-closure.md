source per colourway with CSS `hue-rotate`, implementing an owner ruling (Round 2, item 4): flame
hue at least 90 degrees from the backdrop hue, never green-on-green.

| colourway | filter | from GREEN, hue 110 | from the candidate, hue 202 |

# WORKSTREAM 5 - THE SHADOW INSERTION POINT

**Now exact, and my R105 statement of it was slightly off.** The breathing animation is on the
LAYER, not the image: `.car-layer` carries `animation: car-hover 6s` and `.char-layer` carries
`animation: char-idle 5s`, both animating `transform`. A shadow inside either bobs with its hero.

**`.scene-group` is the correct parent.** It is `position:absolute; inset:0; pointer-events:none`
and its own comment calls it a "Non-stacking wrapper: no z-index/transform of its own".

```svelte
<div class="scene-group">
  <!-- NEW: siblings of the animated layers, so they stay planted -->
  <img class="car-shadow"  src="{$themeAssets.assetBase}/ui/car_contact_shadow.png"  alt="" />
  <img class="char-shadow" src="{$themeAssets.assetBase}/ui/robot_contact_shadow.png" alt="" />
  <div class="car-layer">  ... </div>
  <div class="char-layer"> ... </div>
</div>
```

```css
.car-shadow, .char-shadow { position:absolute; pointer-events:none; }
.car-shadow  { left:-30px; bottom:10px; width:860px; z-index:7;  /* under .car-layer  z8  */ }
.char-shadow { /* match .char-layer's left/bottom/width */        z-index:29; /* under z30 */ }
```

**NOT implemented, deliberately.** Positioning a 680x240 shadow under a character drawn
`object-fit: contain` inside an 860px box needs visual iteration that cannot be verified
headlessly, and a guessed offset produces a shadow floating beside the feet rather than under
them. The art also has no manifest row, so it could not be committed with its wiring even if
placed. **This is the brief's own stated fallback: a precise next-brief snippet.**
