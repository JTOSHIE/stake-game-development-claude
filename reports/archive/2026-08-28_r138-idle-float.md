## WORKSTREAM 2: the float

`char-idle` restored on `.char-layer` at a measured small amplitude rather than
as a new pendulum: **translateY only, 3px over 5s ease-in-out** - half the
car's amplitude, inside the brief's 2 to 4px and 4 to 6s bands, and 5s kept
deliberately non-harmonic with the car's 6s so the two never phase-lock. The
old rule's rotate(0.6deg) and scale(1.015) stay dead (both fenced), and the
`.char-idle-strip` disable rule is deleted with the restore. The pose cannot
tick by construction: the travel is one level above the sprite, and HeroIdle's
sheet still holds frame 01 with no idle animation. The optional contact-shadow
was considered and refused: the hero's drop-shadow lives on `.hero-body` and is
computed from its whole subtree (the R129 double-shadow lesson), a
counter-phase glow for a 3px bob is imperceptible, and the car's own filter
shadow rides its bob exactly as the hero's now does.

Proved over 11.5s live: wrapper travels 3.0px on a 5.0s period, sheet
animation-name stays `none` and background-position-x stays `0px` at every
sample, body transform stays `none` (no punch).

## WORKSTREAM 3: the dissolve, shipped rather than refused

Measured first, on the shipped sheets at render size: the win strip's adjacent
frames differ by up to **18.6%** silhouette and the brace's by **19.5%**, the
same magnitude as the old idle tick's 18.75% worst step, played at 8 to 10fps.
That is the flipbook read. The legs carry **0.0 to 0.2%** of every worst step -
the stance is planted and the whole unfold lives in arms, chest and head - so
the brief's "ghosts legs" refusal case is structurally absent, and the apex
fist stays inside the box on both buffers so nothing can clip.

The crossfade: `.hero-cross`, mounted only inside `{#if motion !== 'idle'}`,
rides exactly one frame ahead of the sheet and fades 0 to 1 over each of the
n-1 transitions; the sheet holds the current frame at opacity 1 underneath.
Step boundaries of the two buffers coincide by construction (both divide the
same duration into n-1 intervals), so the handoff instant changes nothing on
screen. Enter and exit still land on rest (frames 01 and n are 0.000%
silhouette from idle rest). The epic hold stays 1.9s, reaching BOTH of the top
buffer's clocks (position 1.9s, fade 126.667ms x 15). The ghost judgement was
made on deterministic worst-case composites - frame i under frame i+1 at 50%,
the exact CSS compositing, judged at twice drawn size for the two widest pairs
(win 7 to 8, brace 4 to 5): one head, one torso, pixel-stable legs, arms
reading as motion blur. No ghosted second body, so the dissolve ships.

**The honest residue, art not code:** the departing edge of each frame still
cuts at the boundary, because the bottom buffer must hold opacity 1 - fading
it would show the backdrop through the body, which is the ghost the brief
forbids. Half of every step is smoothed (the arriving edge); a fully
continuous unfold needs denser drawings, not more CSS.
