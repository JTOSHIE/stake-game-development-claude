## HOW TO READ THIS, and it matters

**Every finding below is REPORTED under protocol rule 16 unless it is marked VERIFIED.**
A REPORTED premise tells you what to CHECK FIRST. It does not tell you what is true. The
agents were instructed to cite file:line and to quote rather than paraphrase, and they
largely did, but a session narrating a finding is not the same as the repository holding
it, and several findings in earlier passes of this project turned out to be wrong.

**Six are marked VERIFIED**, meaning I recounted them myself against the repository after
the agent reported them. Those six are the ones to act on without further checking.
