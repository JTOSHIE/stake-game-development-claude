## What the three results mean together

Under the true per mode costs the three sessions imply opening balances of
exactly **EUR 1,000.00**, **EUR 1,000,000.00** and **EUR 500,000.00**, each to
the cent, each a round platform preset figure, with a residual of 0.00 in all
three.

Under the reading that the platform COST column is what the wallet was charged,
the same three sessions imply EUR 291.75, EUR 900,250.00 and EUR 499,250.00.

Three separate sessions, on two separate days, at three separate bet levels, all
landing on round preset figures under one reading and on arbitrary figures under
the other, is a discriminating test rather than a confirmation of what was
already believed. The wallet charges 1.25x for `antelite` and 100x for `bonus`.

## What does not close, stated rather than forced

- **Frame 01 to frame 02 does not join, and it is not meant to.** Frame 01 closes
  at EUR 1,167,050.00 at 02:23:45 and frame 02's session opens at EUR 500,000.00
  around 02:26:06. The Bets counter also restarts, from `Bets 10` to `Bets 11`
  with an entirely fresh row set, and frame 02's Network panel shows five
  `authenticate` calls. The wallet was re-seeded between the two frames. Two
  sessions, not one broken one.
- **Frame 02 to frame 03 is open.** EUR 501,870.00 at 02:27:04 to EUR 479,710.00
  at 02:30:41, a fall of EUR 22,160.00, with the intervening rows not in
  evidence: frame 05's Bets panel is clipped to the TIME column alone and shows
  eighteen rows all inside the 02:27 minute. The fall is unremarkable at
  EUR 1,250.00 a spin over three minutes and thirty seven seconds, but
  unremarkable is not reconciled, and per convention (l.6) it is left open.
  One capture closes it: the Bets panel with its COST and PAYOUT columns visible
  at the same moment as the BALANCE readout.
