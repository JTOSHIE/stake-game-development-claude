## STT-MOBILEL-3-04 HIGH The max win hero sets `5,000`, `×` and `BET` at three different sizes, on two different paddings, separated by a gap narrower than a word space

- Frames: `/Users/jt/math-sdk/.evidence-scratch/stream-test-upscaled-1600/256_mobile-l_transition_maxwin_overlay_fade.png`, `/Users/jt/math-sdk/.evidence-scratch/stream-test-upscaled-1600/257_mobile-l_maxwin_celebration.png`
- Claim: the hero of the biggest moment in the game reads as the single token `5,000×BET`. Three sibling spans with no whitespace between them, `frontend/src/lib/components/MaxWinCelebration.svelte:155` (``` <span class="c1-max-mult fs-num">5,000</span><span class="c1-max-x">×</span> ```) and `:159` (``` <span class="c1-max-betlabel">{t($locale, 'bet', localeMode)}</span> ```), inside a wrapper declared at `:295-300` as ``` display: flex; align-items: baseline; gap: 0.1em ```. Two things follow that a native thumbnail could not resolve:
  1. **Three sizes, and this viewport is the worst case.** The `@media (max-width: 500px)` block at `:365-369` sets `.c1-max-mult` to `50px` and `.c1-max-betlabel` to `13px` while `.c1-max-x` goes to `24px`. So on this 425px frame the multiplication sign is **24px against a 13px unit it belongs to and a 50px figure it operates on**: the sign is nearly twice the size of the word it joins.
  2. **Neither part sits on the baseline the wrapper declares.** `.c1-max-x` at `:309-315` and `.c1-max-betlabel` at `:316-321` each override the wrapper's `align-items: baseline` with `align-self: flex-end`, and then take **different** `padding-bottom` values, `0.12em` and `0.28em`. At their mobile sizes that is `2.9px` against `3.6px`, so the sign and the unit are bottom-aligned to the flex line rather than to each other or to the figure, and end up on three optical baselines.
  The `gap: 0.1em` resolves against the wrapper's own inherited font size, not the 50px figure, so the same sub-word-space separation is applied between the figure and the sign as between the sign and the unit.
- Resolution note: NEW AT 1600PX for the size and baseline split; the missing space was VISIBLE AT BOTH.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/MaxWinCelebration.svelte:295-300`, `:309-321` and `:365-369` (not locked)
- Proposed fix: set `×` and `BET` at one size on one baseline (drop both `align-self`/`padding-bottom` overrides and let the wrapper's `align-items: baseline` do its job), and put the separation between the sign and the unit with a `margin-left` on `.c1-max-betlabel` rather than sharing one `gap` equally across both joins.
