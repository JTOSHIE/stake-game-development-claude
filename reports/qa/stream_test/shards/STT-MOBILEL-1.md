**One fact settles more of this shard than any finding, so it is stated first.** The whole game is set in ONE family. `app.css:97` declares `--fs-font-display: 'Orbitron', system-ui, sans-serif` and `app.css:98` declares `--fs-font-numeric: 'Orbitron', 'Courier New', monospace`, so both tokens resolve to the same face, and `app.css:99` puts the display token on the root so everything inherits it. Only `@fontsource/orbitron/400.css`, `/700.css` and `/900.css` are loaded (`main.ts:2-4`). There is no second brand face and no body face in this build. Every "different font" reading in the native pass, including one I wrote myself at step 2, is therefore a reading of casing, tracking, weight or size, and I have rewritten each of them as that. Two of my own step-2 claims did not survive it and are recorded as withdrawn rather than shipped.
