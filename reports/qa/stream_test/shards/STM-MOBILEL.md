## STM-MOBILEL-01 STREAM The reel window is transparent to the background scene for the first part of every spin
- Frames: `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/214_mobile-l_transition_reels_accelerating.png`. Endpoints either side: `213_mobile-l_base_idle.png` (full 5x4 grid) and `215_mobile-l_transition_reels_full_speed.png` (full 5x4 grid).
- Claim: at the manifest's `Reels accelerating, about 250ms after spin press`, the reel viewport is not filled. Reel 1 carries three symbol tiles, reel 2 carries two, reel 3 carries one, and everything below each stack is the parallax scene painted straight through: the magenta neon chevron artwork and the rain streaked city are legible inside the reel window between the cyan frame's left edge and reel 3. Each column ends in a hard tile edge that lines up with no cell boundary, so a viewer sees three staggered stumps of reel over open scenery. Confirmed on a 2x nearest neighbour crop of `x 8..258, y 140..440` of frame 214. Neither endpoint contains a hole: 213 and 215 both render 20 opaque cells.
  The geometry is exact and comes from the source. The shipping reel mode is `drop` (`GameGrid.svelte:10-12`). `_dropReel` sets `const DROP_H = 520` and `const startY = REST_Y - DROP_H` (`GameGrid.svelte:499,501`) with `REST_Y = -104` (`:75`), so the strip starts at `translateY(-624px)`. The strip is `STRIP = ROWS + 3`, seven slots (`:74`), each `100px` tall with a `4px` gap (`:1253-1254`, `:1244`), so it is `724px` long and its bottom edge sits at `-624 + 724 = +100`. The column viewport `.symbol-col` is `412px` tall (`:1228`). **So `312` of the viewport's `412` pixels, 75.7 per cent of the reel window, carries no tile at the instant the drop begins**, and `delayMs` staggers the columns so they uncover by different amounts, which is the staircase the frame shows. Neither `.symbol-col` (`GameGrid.svelte:1225-1232`, which has `overflow: hidden` and no `background`) nor `.grid-container` (`:1204-1214`, `box-shadow` only) declares a fill, so the uncovered region composites to the scene layer behind the grid.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/GameGrid.svelte:1225-1232` (the `.symbol-col` rule), with the geometry that causes it at `:74` and `:499-501`.
- Proposed fix: give `.symbol-col` an opaque plate, `background: #1c1d2e;`, the full alpha form of the tile tone already at `GameGrid.svelte:1259` (`rgba(28, 29, 46, 0.88)`), so an uncovered strip region reads as an empty reel slot instead of the city. One property, no geometry change. The larger alternative, raising `STRIP` until the strip is at least `DROP_H + 412` long, is not needed if the window is opaque.

## STM-MOBILEL-02 STREAM The MAX WIN celebration is fully drawn before its backdrop covers the game, so the HUD money pods read through it at full brightness
- Frames: `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/256_mobile-l_transition_maxwin_overlay_fade.png`. Endpoints: `255_mobile-l_post_feature_base.png` (no overlay) and `257_mobile-l_maxwin_celebration.png` (settled overlay).
- Claim: at `Max win overlay mid-fade-in` the celebration content is already at full strength (`MAX WIN REACHED!`, `5,000`, the three stars and the `COLLECT` button all solid) while the surface meant to hide the game has not arrived. Measured over the WIN pod digit band `x 265..375, y 618..640`: peak luminance `229` in frame 256, against `222` in frame 255 where there is no overlay at all, and `147` in the settled frame 257. The `$5,000.00` readout is therefore as bright underneath the celebration as it is with no celebration on screen. `$50,000.00`, the `BALANCE` and `WIN` labels and the `$1.00` bet value are all legible through the overlay in the same frame. Separately the overlay's own line `PRESS COLLECT OR HIT ENTER TO CONTINUE` is painted over the game's `FEATURES` button label, two strings occupying overlapping rows at `y 528..556`, confirmed on a 1.9x brightened crop of `y 510..690`.
  Both halves come from one rule. `.c1-max` carries `animation: c1-fadein 0.55s ease both` (`MaxWinCelebration.svelte:201`) with `@keyframes c1-fadein { from { opacity: 0; } to { opacity: 1; } }` (`:203`), and the content sits inside it at `z-index: 2` (`.c1-max-content`, `:243-244`), so the opacity ramp applies to backdrop and content as one group: for `0.55` of a second the game shows through the celebration. The settled residue is a second, separate cause: the backdrop gradient never reaches full alpha, `rgba(20, 8, 50, 0.97)` and `rgba(6, 4, 20, 0.99)` (`:196-197`), which is why frame 257 still ghosts at peak `147`.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/MaxWinCelebration.svelte:196-197` and `:201,203`.
- Proposed fix: two small edits. Take the two gradient stops to alpha `1` so the settled overlay is opaque; and move `c1-fadein` off `.c1-max` onto a dedicated full bleed backdrop child so the backdrop reaches full cover first, giving `.c1-max-content` its own shorter fade delayed behind it. The celebration then never reveals a figure the game is still showing underneath.

## STM-MOBILEL-03 HIGH Mid-fade, the game's `FEATURES` button label is printed across the rules card's `Continue` button
- Frames: `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/210_mobile-l_transition_splash_to_rules.png`. Endpoint: `211_mobile-l_intro_rules.png`.
- Claim: during the splash to rules fade the word `FEATURES` and its icon are rendered on the face of the rules card's cyan `Continue` pill, immediately above the word `Continue`, so two strings of text occupy the primary call to action at once. Confirmed on a 2x brightened crop of `x 14..414, y 520..630` of frame 210, where `FEATURES` reads clearly across the pill; the same crop of the settled frame 211 shows a clean pill. The HUD money readouts `$50,000.00` and `$0.00` are legible through the card body in the same crop of 210 and are absent from the same crop of 211. Across the band `y 600..812` mean luminance is `10.6` in 210 against `5.8` in the settled 211 and `27.8` in the undimmed `213_mobile-l_base_idle.png`.
  Mechanism, from the source: `.intro-card` carries `animation: intro-card-in 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.2, 0.4, 1) both` (`IntroSplash.svelte:79`) and `@keyframes intro-card-in` ramps `opacity` from `0` as well as `transform` from `scale(0.92)` (`:148`). The card's `Continue` button is a child of the card, so it inherits that group opacity, while the backdrop behind it is on its own separate ramp, `intro-fade-in 0.35s ease both` over `background: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.86)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(3px)` (`:41-49`, `:147`). Two independent opacity ramps over a still bright game is exactly the composite the frame shows.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/IntroSplash.svelte:148`, with the two ramps at `:49` and `:79`.
- Proposed fix: drop `opacity` from `intro-card-in` so the card animates on `transform` only and is opaque from its first painted frame, leaving the backdrop alone to carry the fade. Nothing behind can then reach the `Continue` button face. One keyframe edit; the `prefers-reduced-motion` branch at `:151` is unaffected.

## STM-MOBILEL-05 MEDIUM Seven of the eighteen assigned transition captures landed on a settled state, so those transitions are unaudited, and the delays that caused it are in the harness
- Frames: `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/208_mobile-l_transition_splash_entrance.png`, `212_mobile-l_transition_rules_to_base.png`, `223_mobile-l_transition_menu_opening.png`, `226_mobile-l_transition_paytable_opening.png`, `237_mobile-l_transition_paytable_closing.png`, `247_mobile-l_transition_feature_starting.png`, `254_mobile-l_transition_feature_exit.png`.
- Claim: measured with `ffmpeg -lavfi psnr` against the settled frame each is meant to be moving towards or away from, these captures sit at or below the frame to frame noise of two settled frames of the same state. `226` against `227_mobile-l_paytable_top.png` is `psnr_avg 59.05`, `mse_avg 0.08`, visually identical including the yellow left hand scroll rail and the identical bottom clipping. `247` against `248_mobile-l_feature_run_1.png` is `psnr_avg 38.47`, `mse_avg 9.24`. `212` against `213_mobile-l_base_idle.png` is `mse_avg 57.32` and `223` against `224_mobile-l_hud_menu.png` is `mse_avg 26.44`; for scale, two consecutive settled feature frames 252 and 253 differ by `mse_avg 57.96` purely from animated rain and particles. `208` against `209_mobile-l_splash.png` is `mse_avg 5.51`. `237` and `254` both show a fully settled base game with no trace of the surface they are meant to be dismissing. The same harness did catch genuine mid states elsewhere in this session: `210` against `211` is `mse_avg 297.86`, `241` against `242` is `1044.88`, `243` against `244` is `1105.34`, `256` against `257` is `1054.14`.
  The cause is readable in the harness, which I read as text and did not run. `frontend/scripts/stream_test_capture.mjs:299` waits `await settle(page, 160)` before shooting `transition_menu_opening`; `:315` waits `await settle(page, 200)` before `transition_paytable_opening`; `:409` waits `await settle(page, 300)` before `transition_feature_starting`. Against the animation durations now known from the source, `0.35s` and `0.4s` on the intro pair (`IntroSplash.svelte:49,79`) and `0.55s` on the max win overlay (`MaxWinCelebration.svelte:201`), a delay of `160` to `300` ms is long enough to miss anything shorter than about a third of a second.
  Stated as an evidence gap, not as a claim that those animations are missing. Either they are shorter than the delay or they are hard cuts, and only the second would be a stream visible defect, so the honest disposition is that the paytable open and close, the HUD menu open, the rules dismissal, the splash entrance, the feature start and the feature exit are not audited for motion residue on `mobile-l`.
- Where fixable: `frontend/scripts/stream_test_capture.mjs:299,315,409` and the sibling `settle` calls on the other transition shots. Not frontend rendering source.
- Proposed fix: shoot each transition at a fraction of its known duration rather than at a fixed constant, by reading the duration from the component and halving it, or shoot two frames per transition at roughly `0.25` and `0.6` of it. A cheaper interim: drop the three constants above to about `70` ms so a `0.35s` animation is caught at a fifth rather than at half.

## STM-MOBILEL-06 MEDIUM The feature entry gate is still up across the frame that should show it dismissing and the first two frames that claim the feature is in flight, and the harness cannot tell
- Frames: `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/247_mobile-l_transition_feature_starting.png`, with `248_mobile-l_feature_run_1.png` and `249_mobile-l_feature_run_2.png` opened as endpoints.
- Claim: `MANIFEST.json` describes 247 as `Feature starting, entry card dismissing` and 248 and 249 as `Overdrive free spins in flight, interval frame 1 of 6` and `2 of 6`. All three render the `OVERDRIVE FREE SPINS` entry card with its gauge, `+16 FREE SPINS` and the `TAP TO CONTINUE` button at full opacity, and all three carry the identical win line strip `SCATTER  x5  5 ways  $10.00` behind the card, so nothing behind the card advanced either. `247` against `248` is `psnr_avg 38.47`, a smaller difference than any pair of consecutive settled feature frames in this session (`248` to `249` is `33.89`, `249` to `250` is `32.10`, `250` to `251` is `32.11`). The feature did run, because 254 and 255 show `WIN $318.64` and `$363.89`, so the gate cleared somewhere after 249.
  The harness cannot detect this, and that is the part worth fixing. `frontend/scripts/stream_test_capture.mjs:408` calls `await clickLive(page, '[data-testid="entry-continue"]')` and does not check the result, and `clickLive` itself (`:193-201`) swallows the click with `await el.click({ timeout }).catch(() => {})` and then returns `true` on the first visible match regardless of whether the click landed. A dismissal that silently failed is therefore indistinguishable from one that worked, and six frames get written and labelled `in flight` while showing a gate.
- Where fixable: `frontend/scripts/stream_test_capture.mjs:199` (the swallowed click) and `:408` (the unchecked call site). Not frontend rendering source.
- Proposed fix: have `clickLive` return `false` when the click throws instead of swallowing it, and at `:408` assert the gate is gone before entering the six frame loop, for example by waiting for `[data-testid="entry-continue"]` to detach and failing the capture loudly if it does not. This is convention (p) applied to a capture script: a harness that cannot fail cannot be evidence.

## Explicit absences, signed

I looked for each of the following on all 18 assigned frames and found none. Each line says what was checked so the absence is checkable rather than asserted.

- **No element caught mid-teleport.** No surface sits at a position that is neither its start nor its end. Checked by locating the reel frame edges numerically on 245, 246, 258 and 259 and the HUD pod edges by eye on all 18.
- **No unintended whole view displacement.** Frame 258 does carry one: the reel frame's top edge is at `y=122` against `y=119` in 259, 246 and 245, and its right edge at `x=420` against `x=424`, with the `FUTURE SPINNER` wordmark, the HUD pods and the button row all moving with it. I opened this as a finding and then withdrew it: it is the intended screen shake, fired at `App.svelte:492` (`if (script.triggered) triggerShake()`), bound at `:1701` (`class:shake={shakeActive}`) and keyframed at `:2212`, and frame 259 confirms a feature triggered at that moment (`+8 FREE SPINS`). An intended effect is not a defect and is not reported as one.
- **No ghost of a dismissed surface.** `237_mobile-l_transition_paytable_closing.png` and `254_mobile-l_transition_feature_exit.png` carry no remnant of the paytable or of the free spins chrome; both are clean settled base frames. 258 carries no remnant of the max win overlay.
- **No wrong z-order on the buy dialogs.** `241_mobile-l_transition_dialog_buy_overdrive_opening.png` and `243_mobile-l_transition_dialog_nitro_overdrive_opening.png` differ from their settled endpoints only by a small uniform scale and slight text softness (`mse_avg 1044.88` and `1105.34`), consistent with a scale and fade in. Nothing behind them reads through their bodies and no game text lands on `CANCEL` or `BUY`.
- **No backdrop blur on the wrong layer.** Checked the modal surfaces at 210, 226, 239, 241, 243, 245 and 256 against their endpoints. Where a dimming or blurring layer exists it is behind its surface, not in front of it; `IntroSplash.svelte:47` puts `backdrop-filter: blur(3px)` on `.intro-backdrop`, which is correct. The defect at 210 is the card passing text through, not the blur being misplaced.
- **No shadow or glow left behind by a moved element.** Checked around the reel frame, the SPIN button, the FEATURES bar and the modal cards on all 18 frames. The only glow that changes independently is the `TAP TO CONTINUE` halo at 245 against 246, a pulse on a stationary button, not a residue.
- **No two surfaces overlapping wrongly on the reel grid during the big win count-up.** The hard horizontal band edges that cut the top and bottom symbol rows in 220 and 221 are present identically in the settled 222, so they belong to a settled state lens and not to this one.
- **A candidate I opened and then withdrew, recorded so the marshal knows it was checked rather than missed.** On first reading of `223_mobile-l_transition_menu_opening.png` I read the balance digits as ghosting through the HUD menu panel and colliding with the `Session` row. A 2.4x brightened crop of `x 10..290, y 580..660` shows the panel body is opaque and the digits I had read sit outside its right edge at about `x 281`. The same band is numerically identical in the transition and settled frames (mean `13.7`, peak `245` in both 223 and 224). Not a finding.
- **Nothing new on the `x` against `×` glyph class from my frames.** The `xBET` unit on the max win overlay at 256 and 257 renders too small on a `425x812` viewport for me to call the codepoint safely from the frame, so I am not calling it. `MaxWinCelebration.svelte:151-155` is named in `LEDGER.md` MID-02 as already carrying `×`; the marshal should read the source rather than take a reading from this frame in either direction.
