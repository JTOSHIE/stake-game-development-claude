## STT-POPOUTL-B-01 HIGH The Buy Overdrive dialog's last line of prose is sliced through the letterforms by the sticky stats row

- Frames: `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/139_popout-l_dialog_buy_overdrive.png`, `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/138_popout-l_transition_dialog_buy_overdrive_opening.png`
- Claim: the third `WHAT YOU GET` paragraph shows only `The Overdrive meter starts at 1× and rises +1× after every` and is then cut horizontally through the middle of the glyphs, descenders gone, no terminating full stop and no ellipsis. The full string is `rulesOverdriveMeter` at `frontend/src/lib/i18n/translations.ts:1541`: `'The Overdrive meter starts at 1× and rises +1× after every winning free spin, multiplying all later wins. It never resets during the feature.'` so eleven words plus two sentences of it never reach the player. The cause is `.buy-stats-row` at `frontend/src/lib/components/BuyBonus.svelte:229-232`, which is `position: sticky; bottom: 0; z-index: 2` over an opaque `background: linear-gradient(180deg, #1a2236, #080c16)` (`:227`). The dialog's own scroll box is `max-height: 90dvh; overflow-y: auto` (`:174`) with no bottom padding reserved, so at 450px viewport height the sticky row parks over the final line at maximum scroll and the text can never be scrolled clear of it. Frame `139` is the held, settled dialog, so this is not a transition artefact. The sibling `NITRO OVERDRIVE` dialog carries a shorter blurb and does not clip (`141`), so two instances of one component disagree on whether their prose survives. This is a non-money string, so it sits outside the TR-115 / TR-086 money-fit class.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/BuyBonus.svelte:174` and `frontend/src/lib/components/BuyBonus.svelte:229-232` (not locked)
- Proposed fix: reserve the sticky row's own height as bottom padding on the scrolling modal box, so the last prose line scrolls clear of it instead of parking permanently underneath.
