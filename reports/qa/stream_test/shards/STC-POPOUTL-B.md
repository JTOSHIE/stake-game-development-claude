**Provenance of the numbers, stated up front so the marshal can weight them.** An
earlier run of this same squad had already written a shard to this path, with its
geometry marked "approx" and read off the rendered frame by eye. This run opened all
26 frames independently and then measured the load-bearing geometry by decoding the
PNGs and scanning luminance, so figures below are pixel-measured unless the line says
otherwise. **Finding ids are held stable from that earlier shard** so any citation of
`STC-POPOUTL-B-nn` elsewhere stays correct: where measurement confirmed a finding the
figures are replaced with measured ones, and where measurement REFUTED it the id is
kept in place and marked `WITHDRAWN` with the measurement that killed it, rather than
being deleted. Two findings were withdrawn on that basis, `B-11` and `B-14`, and one,
`B-12`, changed materially in what it says. Nothing from the earlier run was dropped
silently.

## STC-POPOUTL-B-01 STREAM The buy confirm dialogs offer no confirm and no cancel control, and their body copy is sliced mid-sentence
- Frames: `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/139_popout-l_dialog_buy_overdrive.png`, `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/141_popout-l_dialog_nitro_overdrive.png`, `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/138_popout-l_transition_dialog_buy_overdrive_opening.png`, `reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/140_popout-l_transition_dialog_nitro_overdrive_opening.png`
- Claim: at `800x450` the dialog's outer border closes at y `426` (measured on `139`, the rounded bottom edge of the magenta outline, whose verticals sit at x `172` and x `622`), and the three-cell stat strip (`PRICE` / `RTP` / `MAX WIN`) closes at y `400`. There is no confirm control, no cancel control and no close affordance anywhere inside the panel in any of the four frames. On `139` the last body line reads `The Overdrive meter starts at 1x and rises +1x after every` and is cut horizontally through the glyph row by the top edge of the stat strip, mid-sentence, with no fade, no ellipsis and no scroll indicator. `MANIFEST.json` calls this surface `Buy confirm dialog, price stated up front`, so the frame does not show what it is meant to show: the game asks `Start Overdrive Free Spins now at 100x your bet?`, states `$100.00`, and gives the player nothing to answer with. - Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/BuyConfirmDialog.svelte` (see the file:line note under `Where fixable` for `B-02`; the two share the shape of the fault)
- Proposed fix: keep the stat strip and the action row outside the scrolling region so the panel's fixed furniture is header, strip and buttons, with only the prose scrolling between them.
