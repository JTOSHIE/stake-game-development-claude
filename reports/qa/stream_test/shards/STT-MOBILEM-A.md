  1. **Glyph and order.** `frontend/src/lib/components/WinBreakdown.svelte:92` builds the match count as `` <span class="wb-count">x{current.kind}</span> ``. The `x` is ASCII U+0078 and it LEADS the number. The same quantity for the same symbol is written by `frontend/src/lib/components/PaytableModal.svelte:233-235` as `` <span class="fs-pay-count">3×</span> ``, `4×`, `5×`: multiplication sign U+00D7, TRAILING the number. One game, one quantity, two glyphs and two orders. Frames `273`, `275` and `276` render `L3` `x4`; frame `274` renders `M3` `x5`.
  This is a NEW member of the class, not a re-report. `KNOWN_OPEN` **Q-26** scopes itself to blurb strings and enumerates `1.6x`, `1.25x` twice and `5x`; `LEDGER` **MID-02** adds `WinBanner.svelte:205` and states in terms that Q-26's enumeration is incomplete. `WinBreakdown.svelte:92` is in neither list and is on a surface that appears on every paying spin rather than only on tier wins.
  2. **Unconditional plural.** `frontend/src/lib/components/WinBreakdown.svelte:94` is `` <span class="wb-ways">{current.ways} ways</span> ``, with no singular branch, so a one-way win renders `1 ways`. Frames `273`, `275` and `276` show exactly that: `L3  x4  1 ways  $0.20`.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/WinBreakdown.svelte:92` and `:94` (not locked)
- Proposed fix: line 92 becomes `` {current.kind}× `` to match the paytable's glyph and order; line 94 branches on the count, `{current.ways} {current.ways === 1 ? 'way' : 'ways'}`, routed through the prose layer like the other composed player strings.

## Retracted after source check, recorded rather than deleted

- **RETRACTED: "the paytable writes the multiplier with a letter `x`".** Written from frames `281` to `285` on the reading that `1x`, `3x`, `10x`, `3x`/`4x`/`5x` and `COST 1x` used ASCII `x` while neighbouring strings used `×`. **The source refutes it.** `PaytableModal.svelte:95-97` is `` award: '1×' ``, `'3×'`, `'10×'`; `:233-235` is `3×`, `4×`, `5×`; `:321` is `` {m.cost}× ``; `prose.ts:112` is `1×, 3×, or 10×`; `prose.ts:113` is `5,000×`; `prose.ts:117` is `3 / 4 / 5 = 1× / 3× / 10×`. Every one is U+00D7. At roughly 11px on a 375px capture, U+00D7 and lower-case `x` are not separable by eye, and I called it wrong. The real instance of that class in this range is `WinBreakdown.svelte:92`, finding 01, which I had not suspected from the frames at all.
- **RETRACTED: "the in-grid `WIN!` callout is not set in the brand face".** Written from frame `271` on the apparent narrowness of the `W` and `N`. `frontend/src/lib/components/WinCelebration.svelte:35` renders `{$tr('winFlash')}` and `:56` sets `font-family: var(--fs-font-display)`, so it is the brand display face.
