scope: every frame of the `laptop` session numbered 053 to 078 inclusive, 26 frames, splash through paytable section 06 (bet modes)
frames_read: 26

All pixel figures below are read off the captured PNG at its native 1024x576 and are
approximate to about 5 px unless stated. Every one is anchored to the frame path that
shows it, per the citation rule. Where a step 3 source lookup then produced the design
figure behind a measurement, both are given, and the source figure is the authority.

**One thing this build hands the reader for free, and it is worth stating up front.**
`App.svelte:747-748` records that the whole surface is laid out on a fixed `1280x720`
design stage and scaled by one factor `S = min(vw/1280, vh/720)`. At this viewport that
factor is exactly `0.8`, so every stage unit in the source is `0.8` screen px in my
frames and any measurement here converts to a source figure without inference. Three of
the findings below were measured off the frame first and then landed on the source
number to within about 1 px, which is recorded because it is the corroboration, not
because it is a coincidence.
