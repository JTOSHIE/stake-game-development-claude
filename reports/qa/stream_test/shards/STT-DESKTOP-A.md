## STT-DESKTOP-A-01 HIGH The paytable's bullet lists are set as centred prose with their bullet glyphs stranded at the left margin, because the Vite scaffold's `text-align: center` is still on `#app`

- Frames: /Users/jt/math-sdk/reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/024_desktop_paytable_04_rules.png, /Users/jt/math-sdk/reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/025_desktop_paytable_05_overdrive_free_spins.png, /Users/jt/math-sdk/reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/023_desktop_paytable_03_symbol_payouts.png, against /Users/jt/math-sdk/reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/004_desktop_intro_rules.png and /Users/jt/math-sdk/reports/screens/stream-test-2026-07-28/003_desktop_transition_splash_to_rules.png
- Claim: Every `<li>` in the paytable renders its `›` marker hard against the modal's left content edge while its own text is centre aligned in the row, so the marker is not attached to anything a reader can follow. On frame `024` the six RULES rows put every marker in one column and start their text at six different positions: `Wins pay left to right on adjacent reels starting from reel 1.` begins roughly 330 px right of its own marker, `WILD substitutes for all symbols except SCATTER.` roughly 350 px, and `Malfunctions void all pays and plays.` roughly 400 px. The same defect repeats across the five OVERDRIVE FREE SPINS rows on `025` and the RULES rows at the foot of `023`.

  The mechanism is exactly what the CSS says. `frontend/src/lib/components/PaytableModal.svelte:668-669` sets the list correctly for a left aligned column: `.fs-rules li { ... padding-left: 16px; position: relative; }` with `.fs-rules li::before { content: '›'; position: absolute; left: 0; ... }`. Neither the `li` nor any ancestor inside the component declares `text-align`, so the text inherits from outside the component, and the only such declaration in the tree is the Vite starter's own block at `frontend/src/app.css:139-144`: `#app { max-width: 1280px; margin: 0 auto; padding: 2rem; text-align: center; }`. `frontend/src/main.ts:10-12` mounts the whole game into `document.getElementById('app')`, and `frontend/index.html:22` is `<div id="app"></div>`, so that rule inherits into every surface including the modal. The absolutely positioned marker cannot follow the centred text, so the two separate.

  **The comparison that makes this a defect rather than a house style**: three of those strings are word for word the strings on the intro rules card, `The Overdrive meter starts at 1× and rises +1× after every winning free spin, multiplying all later wins. It never resets during the feature.`, `3 or more Scatters during free spins award +5 free spins.` and `Bonus Buy: pay 100× your bet to start the feature immediately.`, and on frame `004` they are set left aligned, ragged right, with the `›` directly beside the first word. One list, two typesettings, both reachable in one session.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/app.css:143` (the scaffold `text-align: center`), with `frontend/src/lib/components/PaytableModal.svelte:668` as the belt-and-braces site (not locked)
- Proposed fix: delete `text-align: center` from the `#app` block in `app.css` and give the surfaces that actually want centring their own declaration; as an immediate one-liner, add `text-align: left;` to `.fs-rules li` at `PaytableModal.svelte:668`. Note that removing the `#app` line will also re-set the centred headings and captions, so the two-line component fix is the safer order.
