The measurement matches the declared mechanism to the pixel: `:298` is `gap: 0.1em` on `.c1-max-multwrap`, computed against the wrapper's inherited font-size while the three children are set at 96px, 46px and 22px (`:304`, `:311`, `:318`), so 0.1em is one to two pixels. - Resolution note: NEW AT 1600PX for the colour split and for the measured gaps. The bare spacing observation was reachable natively and is reconciled below as STT-MOBILEM-B-03 CONFIRMED; what is new here is that the split is a declared three-token colour hierarchy, not a rendering artefact.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/MaxWinCelebration.svelte:305`, `:312`, `:320` for the colours and `:298` for the gap (not locked). The markup is `:155` and `:159`. A narrow-viewport override that this squad did not open is cited by the superseded shard at `:367-369`; mobile-m's measured glyph heights are consistent with that branch being in force, so any fix must be applied in both rules.

  ```

    H1: 'H1', H2: 'H2', M1: 'M1', M2: 'M2', M3: 'M3',
    L1: 'L1', L2: 'L2', L3: 'L3',
  }
  ```

  Every entry maps a key to itself. `symbolLabel` at `:19-24` routes only `W` and `S` through the string layer (`t('symbolWild')`, `t('symbolScatter')`) and returns the identity for everything else. The comment above it at `:11-14` states the reasoning, *The tier symbols are IDs, identical in every language, so they stay a plain map*, which is a correct statement about LOCALISATION and a wrong one about DISPLAY: it explains why they need no translation, not why a player should see them. `H1` to `L3` are the maths package's paytable ladder, the same one `CLAUDE.md` records under "True game facts" as `Paytable unchanged (H1 22/6/1.5 down to L3 0.65/0.20/0.08 per way)`. A player is being shown a database key on the surface that explains what just paid them. The superseded shard filed only the casing half of this strip (`STT-MOBILEM-B-09`, "mixes an upper-case token ... with a lower-case unit word") and did not record that two of those tokens are not words.
- Resolution note: NEW AT 1600PX. At native 375x667 the strip is a single line of roughly 7px type; the difference between a symbol NAME and a symbol CODE is exactly the distinction a thumbnail cannot support.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/WinBreakdown.svelte:15-24` (not locked)
- Proposed fix: give the eight tier symbols display names in the string layer beside `symbolWild` and `symbolScatter`, and make `SYMBOL_IDS` a real map rather than an identity. Note for the marshal, because it changes the size of the job: `STT-MOBILEM-A-02` reports the scatter rendering as `SCAT` on the paytable card at frame `282`, so the display-name source this fix would wire to may itself be carrying truncated ids. ## Step 3 corrections: two step 2 findings WITHDRAWN

Both were written into the step 2 shard, both were plausible at 1600px, and both
are refuted by source. They are recorded here in full so the marshal can see the
false-positive rate of this pass rather than only its yield.

- **WITHDRAWN, was STT-MOBILEM-3-03 at step 2: "the same figure `5,000×` is set in two different numeral faces".** The step 2 claim was that the zeros on the max win hero (`309`) and in every HUD money pod carry an internal diagonal bar while the zeros of `$400.00` and `5,000×` in the buy dialog stat strip (`296`) are plain unbarred bowls, implying a family break on the most repeated number in the game. **REFUTED.** `frontend/src/lib/components/BuyBonus.svelte:179` sets `font-family: var(--fs-font-display)` on `.buy-modal`, and `.buy-stat-val` at `:240` declares no family of its own, so it inherits it. `frontend/src/lib/components/MaxWinCelebration.svelte:302` sets the same `var(--fs-font-display)` on `.c1-max-mult`. One family, two surfaces. The visible difference is size and weight, not face: the hero is `font-size: 96px; font-weight: 900` (`:303-304`) with a heavy `text-shadow` glow at `:307`, while the stat value is `font-size: 0.92rem; font-weight: 700` (`:240`), about 15px, at which size the zero's internal detail does not resolve in a capture taken at 375px and then upscaled. **This is the trap the 1600px re-run itself can fall into: upscaling makes small type legible, it does not make it accurate.** A resampled 15px glyph is still a 15px glyph, and reading fine face detail out of one is not supported by the evidence.
- **WITHDRAWN, was STT-MOBILEM-3-04 at step 2: "the NITRO dialog headline may write its multiplier with a letter `x`".** The step 2 claim was that `Start Overdrive Free Spins now at 400x your bet?` on `295`/`296` might be a fifth Q-26 site, filed with its uncertainty stated. **REFUTED.** The string is `frontend/src/lib/i18n/translations.ts:1537`, `buyConfirmBody: 'Start Overdrive Free Spins now at {cost}× your bet?'`, and the glyph is U+00D7.
