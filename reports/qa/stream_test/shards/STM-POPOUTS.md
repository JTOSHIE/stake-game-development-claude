## STM-POPOUTS-01 STREAM The reel window goes transparent 250 ms into the spin and shows the street scene through the board, while two of five reels are still frozen on the previous result

- Frames: `/Users/jt/math-sdk/.evidence-scratch/stream-test-upscaled-1600/163_popout-s_transition_reels_accelerating.png`. Endpoints: `162_popout-s_base_idle.png` before, `164_popout-s_transition_reels_full_speed.png` and `165_popout-s_dead_spin_1_settled.png` after.
- Claim: at the moment the manifest calls `Reels accelerating, about 250ms after spin press`, the reel window has no opaque backing. Through reels 1, 2 and 3 a viewer sees the scene layer directly: the car's bonnet and windscreen, the wet road surface, the pink and cyan hoardings and the vertical building edges, all in full colour and full focus, framed by the cyan neon reel surround as though the board were a window. Second, reels 4 and 5 still carry the full four tiles of the PREVIOUS result, the identical piston symbol in all eight cells, in the identical cell positions as `162_popout-s_base_idle.png`. So at 250 ms the board reads as three empty holes beside two columns that have not started.
  Source gives the mechanism exactly. The only opaque paint anywhere in the reel stack is on the individual tile, `frontend/src/lib/components/GameGrid.svelte:1259`, `background: rgba(28, 29, 46, 0.88)`. - Resolution note: VISIBLE AT BOTH. This is a gross layout failure and it was resolvable at `400x225`. It is filed as new because **the superseded native shard did not report it at all**, on its own assigned frame, while eleven other squads reported the same class across seven viewports (ledger cluster 1). The native shard used frame 163 only as a PSNR control for its `STM-POPOUTS-05`.
- Where fixable: `frontend/src/lib/components/GameGrid.svelte:1204-1214` and `:1224-1232` (not locked). The tile alpha is at `:1259`.
- Proposed fix: give `.symbol-col` an opaque board fill so a gap in the travelling strip can never reveal the scene, which is one declaration and is preferable to `.grid-container` because it preserves the 4px inter-column gutters the design uses. Then decide separately whether `.symbol-cell`'s `0.88` is intended; if the plate is meant to read as glass over the board it is now correct, and if it was meant to be opaque it should be `1`. Do not fix this by slowing the spin: the transparency is present whenever a strip is short of its viewport, not only at 250 ms.
