- Claim: The ticker renders the scatter award as `SCATTER` `x5` `5 ways` `$10.00`, that is it pushes a scatter through the ways-win sentence template and tells the player the award came from `5 ways`. The template is unconditional: `frontend/src/lib/components/WinBreakdown.svelte:92-95` emits symbol, `x{current.kind}`, `{current.ways} ways` and the pay for every row alike, with no branch for a scatter. - Where fixable: frontend/src/lib/components/WinBreakdown.svelte:92-95 (not locked)
- Proposed fix: branch the row on the scatter symbol and drop the ways token for it, for example render symbol, count and pay only; escalate the final wording per convention (l.8) because the line restates a pay rule.
