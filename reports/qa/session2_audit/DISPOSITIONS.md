## THE THREE-SEAT PANELS EARNED THEIR COST, measured

All 21 player-visible STREAM clusters were read by three independent seats.

- **8 unanimous**, 13 SPLIT between seats.
- **S2-C018 was STRUCK 2 to 1.** Two seats refuted it; one returned CONFIRMED_WRONG_CAUSE. A
  single-seat verification would have kept it as a live finding.
- **27 clusters are UPHELD BUT WITH AN UNSOUND CAUSE.** The defect is real and the stated cause
  is not. **Do not act on the recorded cause for any of these.** That is Session 1's failure
  mode still present, and it is concentrated exactly where a finding asserted a reason rather
  than a fact.

**A split panel is information, not noise.** Where seats disagreed the disposition takes the
worst supported reading rather than averaging, because the cost of dropping a real
player-visible defect exceeds the cost of investigating one that turns out to be smaller.

## WHY THE CONFIRMATION RATE IS SO MUCH HIGHER THAN SESSION 1's

Session 1 measured 5 of 26 clusters, 19 per cent, surviving a hostile read. Here 118 of 126 are
upheld in some form. **This is not because these squads were better.**

Most findings in this session take the form "this requirement has no proof path that would fail
if it were broken". That is a CHECKABLE FACT: a verifier opens `checks.yml`, searches the 20
test files, and either finds the assertion or does not. Session 1 was verifying DIAGNOSES of
visual defects, which are inferences about cause, and inferences are where the 81 per cent
failure lived.

**The 27 CONFIRMED_WRONG_CAUSE rows are the control group that proves it**: they are precisely
the findings that did assert a cause, and they failed at close to Session 1's rate.

The generalisable rule: **a claim-a-path task verifies far better than a diagnose-a-defect task,
so the two should be budgeted and trusted differently.**
