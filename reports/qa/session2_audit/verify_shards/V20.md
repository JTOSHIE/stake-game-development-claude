## PER CLUSTER REASONING

**S2-C219, REFUTED.** The observation is exactly true, and I confirmed both halves of it
by opening both files. `currency.ts:211` hardcodes 2 decimals (0 for zero-decimal
currencies), the earlier platform-metadata branch at `currency.ts:188` does the same via
`display.decimals ?? local?.decimals ?? (ZERO_DECIMAL.has(code) ? 0 : 2)`, and
`formatBalance(micros, currencyCode, localeTag, display)` takes no bet-size argument, so
`HudOverlay.svelte:366` could not pass one. There is no 3-place or 4-place path anywhere in
`frontend/src`: `grep -rn "0\.001" frontend/src/lib` returns nothing and the only three
hits for "precision" are prose comments about the TR-066 abbreviation rule.

The finding still does not stand, for two independent reasons.

First, the requirement as worded is a RESTRICTION, and the code cannot breach it.
REGISTER.md:196 reads "Show the extra decimal places only while the base bet is under
$0.10; above that, use normal currency precision." A game that shows no extra decimal
places anywhere never shows them above $0.10, and its behaviour above $0.10 (2 places) is
affirmatively what the row's second clause demands. Both clauses are satisfied, vacuously
for the first and actually for the second. The status that fits is NOT_APPLICABLE pending
REQ-125, not NOT_MET.

Second, the source line is explicitly discretionary. `rgs.md:297` puts the display of
these values "at the discrecion of the publisher" and the sub-$0.10 gating is what "is
reccomonded". The one imperative in that line is the exact-win-amount rule, which is
REQ-127, and the precision arithmetic is at `rgs.md:295`, which is REQ-125. Both are
already separately clustered, at S2-C015 and S2-C014 respectively, with S2-C062 carrying
REQ-124. So S2-C219 adds no distinct defect to the ledger: it is the same single line of
code (`currency.ts:211`) counted a third time under the one row of the four whose text the
code does not actually violate. Its raising shard says so in its own prose, which is the
cleanest possible refutation and is why I have not hedged this to
CONFIRMED_WRONG_CAUSE: there is no separate defect here whose cause needs restating.

Not INCOMPLETE. I swept the class the cluster belongs to and it is closed at ledger level.
Money formatting is genuinely centralised: `grep -rn "toFixed" frontend/src/lib/components`
returns eleven hits and none is money (transforms, gauges, rain timings, and
`WinPod.svelte:6`'s `$winMultiplier.toFixed(1)`, a multiplier), with
`WinPod.svelte:7` recording "Was `$winAmount.toFixed(2)`, the last money `.toFixed` in
frontend/src". Thirteen components import `formatBalance`, of which the win surfaces are
HudOverlay, WinPod, WinBanner, WinDisplay, WinBreakdown, FreeSpinsPresentation and
BonusInstrumentColumn. That enumeration is worth handing to whoever fixes S2-C014, because
W20 explicitly said "I did not enumerate every win surface in every layout profile": there
are seven, and they all inherit the single 2dp formatter, so one fix at
`currency.ts` reaches all of them.

Severity. TOO_HIGH even at LOW, because LOW still implies a defect exists. Nothing here is
player visible on its own terms: at the shipped $0.10 floor a minimum 0.08x win renders
`$0.01`, and that overstatement IS player visible, but it is S2-C015's finding (REQ-127)
and grading it twice inflates the register's NOT_MET count. If S2-C219 is kept at all it
should be kept as a pointer row to S2-C014, not as work.

One lead I chased and dismissed rather than leaving it implied. `HudOverlay.svelte:367`
computes a second string, `winCompact = formatBalanceCompact(...)`, and
`formatBalanceCompact` uses `maximumSignificantDigits: COMPACT_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` (4)
with no fixed-fraction floor, so for a sub-cent value it carries MORE precision than the
full formatter: 0.008 survives `truncateToSignificant(0.008, 4)` intact and would render
`$0.008` where `winLabel` renders `$0.01`. That is a real divergence in the abstract but it
is not reachable by a player, because `fitMoney` at `HudOverlay.svelte:609`
(`use:fitMoney={{ full: winLabel, compact: winCompact }}`) selects the compact string only
when the full one cannot fit its measured slot, and a five-character string always fits.
Recorded as checked and not a finding.

## NEW FINDINGS FOUND WHILE VERIFYING

**NEW-V20-01. Three register rows in the bet-and-precision family convert explicitly
non-mandatory platform text into imperative requirements, without the normative-force note
that REQ-120 carries for exactly this reason, and one of them is graded HIGH.** This is a
defect in the register rather than in the game, and it matters because these statuses are
what set fix order.

The register knows how to flag a weak source. `reports/qa/compliance_register/REGISTER.md:190`
does it explicitly: "Normative force is weaker than the other rows here: the page offers
the functions as help rather than mandating them, and the obligation is the display format
they produce." No such note appears on any of the following.

- `REGISTER.md:193` REQ-123, ARTEFACT / vis YES, cited to `rgs.md:291`: "Drive the
  player-facing bet ladder from the betLevels array in the authenticate response." The
  cited line reads in full: `docs/stake-engine-live/2026-07-29/rgs.md:291` "It is
  recommended to use the predefined betLevels to guide players." Five lines above it,
  `rgs.md:286` opens the same paragraph "Although bet levels are not mandatory, bets must
  satisfy these conditions:" and the conditions it then makes mandatory are minBet/maxBet
  and stepBet divisibility, which are REQ-121 and REQ-122.
- `REGISTER.md:195` REQ-124, ARTEFACT / vis YES, graded **HIGH** at S2-C062, cited to
  `rgs.md:295`: "Offer bet levels starting at $0.01, $0.02, $0.05 and $0.10 rather than a
  $0.10 floor." Source: "New game submissions should incorporate small denomination bets,
  which are not yet industry standard". A "should" inside the paragraph whose own opening
  line says bet levels are not mandatory is the weakest support of any HIGH row I read, and
  the row does not say so.
- `REGISTER.md:196` REQ-126, the row under verification, cited to `rgs.md:297`, where the
  source hedges twice in one sentence ("at the discrecion of the publisher", "it is
  reccomonded").

Convention (l.7) requires compliance text quoted rather than paraphrased, and the register's
own preamble at `REGISTER.md:14` states "The platform docs are the authority." Paraphrasing
"recommended" as an imperative is the paraphrase that rule exists to prevent, and here it
has produced one refuted cluster (S2-C219) and one HIGH cluster (S2-C062) whose tier rests
on a "should". REQ-127 by contrast is faithfully registered: "it is only a requirement that
wins in-game show exact win amounts" is genuinely mandatory, and that row and REQ-125 are
where the real work is. Suggested disposition: add the REQ-120-style normative-force note
to REQ-123, REQ-124 and REQ-126, re-status REQ-126 as NOT_APPLICABLE pending REQ-125, and
re-check S2-C062's HIGH tier against the "should".
