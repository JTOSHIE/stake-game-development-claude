has a minimum win of >= 0.1x, three points of precision are required: 0.1x * $0.01 = $0.001,
while games with minimum wins <0.1x will require 4 points of precision.`

I tried to refute it on the publisher-discretion sentence in the same paragraph, `How these
win values are displayed is at the discrecion of the publisher, though it is reccomonded that
the extra precions is only displayed with the base bet-size is <$0.10`, and it does not save
the code, for two reasons. The same sentence continues `it is only a requirement that wins
in-game show exact win amounts`, and at the shipped fallback bet of $0.10 a 0.08x win is
$0.008, which `formatBalance` renders as `$0.01`: not exact. Worse, the ladder is not fixed at
$0.10. `betLadder.ts` drives from `rgsBetLevels` and rgs.md:295's preceding sentence asks for
levels `[$0.01, $0.02, $0.05, $0.10, ....]`; at $0.01 a 0.08x win is $0.0008 and two-decimal
formatting renders it as **`$0.00`**, a real win displayed to the player as nothing. That is a
sharper and more embarrassing failure than the one the cluster describes, and every win
readout goes through this one function (`WinDisplay.svelte:88`, `HudOverlay.svelte:366`,
`WinBreakdown.svelte:82`, `FreeSpinsPresentation.svelte:131`). Confirmed at MAJOR, with the
note that the finding as written aims too low.
