## PER CLUSTER REASONING

**S2-C015 (REQ-127, in-game win readouts must be exact).** I tried hard to refute this and could
not. The register's rendering of the requirement is faithful to the source, which matters because
question 5 asks whether the claim ABOUT the requirement is accurate: `rgs.md:297` reads *"The
'balance' value displaying the players bankroll does not need to display more than 2 points of
precision at any time, and it is only a requirement that wins in-game show exact win amounts."*
That is unconditional for wins. The cited lines are exact to the character. The proof-path half is
also sound: I read `win_countup_steady_gate.mjs`'s own header, which states its subject as digit
advance widths (`digit 0 1 2 ... units 834 391 830`, `The fix is per-digit fixed-width boxes on the
AMOUNT ONLY, 0.834em each`), so it is a jitter gate and cannot see a wrong VALUE; it is wired at
`checks.yml:583-584` exactly as cited. `currency.test.ts` contains no assertion of value fidelity:
its decimal assertions run the other way (`eq('JPY is zero-decimal', ...)`, `ok('${code}: no decimal
point', ...)`, `eq('decimals alone are honoured', ...)`). So the requirement has neither a gate nor a
unit test.

Where it falls down is COMPLETENESS, and badly enough that I am marking it INCOMPLETE rather than
CONFIRMED. `frontend/src/lib/utils/currency.ts` exports exactly four things
(`currencySymbolFor`, `isVirtualCurrency`, `formatBalance`, `formatBalanceCompact`): there is no
exact-precision formatter in the codebase at all, so this is not a WinPod defect, it is the absence
of an implementation path. Every win readout routes through the 2dp `formatBalance`:
`HudOverlay.svelte:366` (`winLabel`), `WinBanner.svelte:201` (`amountLabel`),
`WinDisplay.svelte:88`, `WinBreakdown.svelte:82`, `BonusInstrumentColumn.svelte:52`
(`totalWinLabel`), `FreeSpinsPresentation.svelte:131`, and `WinPod.svelte:15`. The finding named the
LEAST player-visible member: WinPod's own comment at `:11-12` says *"Bet Replay is the only consumer
(ReplayMode.svelte)"*. The primary in-game win readout is `HudOverlay.svelte:366` and it is not
mentioned. On severity: at the hardcoded `BET_LEVELS` minimum of 0.10 (`gameStore.ts:7`) the smallest
single-way win is 0.10 x 0.08 = $0.008, which renders `$0.01`, a visible half-cent overstatement,
hence MEDIUM. But `rgs.md:293` says *"New game submissions should incorporate small denomination
bets ... these are levels (in USD): [$0.01, $0.02, $0.05, $0.10, ....]"*, and the live ladder comes
from `rgsBetLevels`, so at a platform-supplied $0.01 level a real win of $0.0008 renders `$0.00`, a
win presented as nothing. That is HIGH and it is reachable without any change to our code.

**S2-C017 (REQ-045, no generic machine-made assets standing in for real art).** This one survived
every refutation I could construct, and it is the strongest finding in my batch. The rule is at
`app.css:157-163` as quoted. My refutation attempts and what happened to each: (1) *scoped Svelte
styles will out-specify it* - only four component files define any focus style at all
(`BetSelector.svelte:283`, `FreeSpinsPresentation.svelte:709`, `HudOverlay.svelte:1057`,
`ResumeOffer.svelte:125`) against thirteen files containing `<button>`, so the great majority of
buttons fall through, including `HudOverlay.svelte:404`, `:407`, `:408`, `:423`, `:483` (the bet
steppers, the bet window, the menu, MAX); (2) *something resets the outline globally* - the only two
`outline: none` declarations in the whole tree are on `.win-panel` and `.audio-slider`, neither a
button; (3) *`app.css` is not loaded* - `main.ts:7` `import './app.css'`. And the selector is
`button:focus`, not only `button:focus-visible`, so it matches after a plain mouse click and persists
until focus moves, which is exactly the "through spin, win and COLLECT" behaviour KNOWN_OPEN
describes. Against a requirement whose own words are *"no default fonts, plain gradients, emoji icons
or border effects standing in for real art"*, a 4px browser-supplied focus ring is the named failure,
not an analogue of it. I would raise this to HIGH.
