### S2-C015 (REQ-127, win readout rounding) INCOMPLETE

Both citations are exact to the line. `currency.ts:211` is the `const decimals = ZERO_DECIMAL.has(code) ? 0 : 2` line and `:218-219` are the two fraction-digit properties; `WinPod.svelte:14-15` are the `$: amtText` pair. The no-proof half also survives: `currency.ts` exports exactly two money formatters, `formatBalance` (`:172`) and `formatBalanceCompact` (`:282`), neither of which can render more than two fraction digits for a fiat code, and `currency.test.ts` contains no assertion mentioning precision, exactness of a win, or a sub-cent value. `win_countup_steady_gate.mjs` is indeed wired at `checks.yml:584` and is indeed about digit-box steadiness, not value fidelity.

The defect is reachable rather than theoretical. `gameStore.ts:7` `export const BET_LEVELS = [0.10, 0.20, 0.50, ...]`, so the bottom rung is $0.10; payouts arrive in centibets, so a win is `bet * n / 100` dollars, which at $0.10 is `n/1000` dollars. The paytable floor is 0.08 per way, giving an exact win of $0.008 that every readout prints as $0.01.

Why INCOMPLETE rather than CONFIRMED: the finding names `WinPod.svelte`, whose own header comment at `:8-13` records that "Bet Replay is the only consumer (ReplayMode.svelte)". The readouts a player actually sees during a live round are `WinDisplay.svelte:88` and `WinBanner.svelte:201`, both performing the identical `formatBalance(Math.round(x * CURRENCY_SCALE), ...)`, and the finding names neither. Naming the shared root in `currency.ts` is correct and does most of the work, but the instance list points at the one surface that is off the live path.

On question 5, the claim ABOUT the requirement: the register row at `REGISTER.md:197` renders REQ-127 as "In-game win readouts must show the exact win amount with no rounding". The source sentence at `docs/stake-engine-live/2026-07-29/rgs.md:297` reads "How these win values are displayed is at the discrecion of the publisher, though it is reccomonded that the extra precions is only displayed with the base bet-size is <$0.10 ... and it is only a requirement that wins in-game show exact win amounts." The requirement clause is real and the row is fair on it, but the row drops the discretion clause and the "<$0.10" recommendation, and our own bottom rung is exactly $0.10, not below it. That does not excuse the rounding, it just means the platform's own recommendation is not the lever here; the bare requirement sentence is. Severity therefore MEDIUM, not HIGH: one cent of divergence, only at the bottom bet level, only when the win is not a whole cent.
