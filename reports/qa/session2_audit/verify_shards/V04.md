I tried three routes to refute the no-proof half and all three failed. (1) `checks.yml` step names:
no replay-blocker step exists, and the ten browser matrix legs (`checks.yml:571-600`) are layout fit,
contrast, splash calm, turbo intensity, paytable card fill, scrim coverage, win count-up steady,
max-win hold, bet selector, preview server. None is replay. (2) The wired `replay rounds` gate asserts
only the absence of the legacy event search, quoted in the table above; nothing in it touches controls,
balance or wallet calls. (3) `grep -rln "isReplay\|replay=true\|start-replay"` across `frontend/scripts`,
`scripts/qa` and every `.test.ts` under services and stores matched exactly one file,
`frontend/scripts/replay_blocker_proof.mjs`, and `grep -rn "replay_blocker" .github/` is empty. Per the
brief's own rule, a gate that exists but is not in `checks.yml` does not refute a no-proof finding.

**Where the claim is PARTLY true rather than fully.** It says the unwired proof "asserts only
`.start-replay` clickability, `.play-again` appearance and `[data-testid="max-win-collect"]`". It asserts
more than that: `replay_blocker_proof.mjs:130-134` checks the EUR-formatted amount on the start button,
`:158-166` checks the win counts up to a non-zero total and carries no `NaN`, and crucially `:110-122`
carries a convention (p) seeded self-test, `fail('seeded interception goes red', 'seeded defect did NOT
block the click; the proof cannot see the defect it exists to catch')`. Understating the script matters
in the fix direction, not against the finding: the cheapest remedy is to wire an existing (p)-compliant
script and add one negative assertion, not to write a gate. And the finding's conclusion is if anything
stronger than stated, because every assertion in that script proves the replay flow WORKS; none proves
that a betting control is ABSENT, which is the actual requirement.

Severity MEDIUM, not high. Three independent guards, the requirement is currently met, and the exposure
is regression rather than a defect a player could see today.
