## PER CLUSTER REASONING

### S2-C022, INCOMPLETE, MEDIUM

The observation is exactly true and I could not shake it. `COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md:19-20` reads
"- **Original IP:** verified. Original designs, produced in-house from vector masters. / No
pre-purchased or third-party licensed content.", and two generation records at HEAD say the
opposite about their own subject: the tile note's Origin row (line 7) and the background
note's Origin row both read "**Externally generated, commissioned by the owner**".

Two things I tried and could not use as a refutation. First, "no pre-purchased or
third-party licensed content" survives on its own terms, because commissioned work is
neither pre-purchased nor licensed in; the false half is specifically "produced in-house
from vector masters". Second, the Watch log below it does not correct the posture line: I
grepped the whole file for `Externally` and `external` and the only hits are line 19, line
93 and line 157, none of which is a correction.

**Why the severity comes down.** The platform requirement behind this (REQ-138, REQ-141) is
original IP and material distinctness, and commissioned art with recorded provenance
satisfies both; CLAUDE.md's Assets convention was amended twice by owner ruling to permit
exactly this class. So nothing here is a compliance breach a reviewer could reject on. What
is defective is the project's own posture document asserting a mechanism that its own
provenance records contradict, which is a record-integrity defect. MEDIUM, and a reviewer
reading the public repository could find it, which is why it is not LOW.

**Why INCOMPLETE.** The finding names two generation notes and stops. The class at HEAD is
wider in both directions:
- a **second** instance of the same overstated claim in the same file,
  `COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md:93` "The in-house vector design system directly addresses the
  AI-generated-assets concern", which the finding does not cite and which fails for the same
  reason;
- at least one **further** externally generated brand asset the finding does not name,
  `design-system/brand/provider_mark/PROVENANCE_g.md:7` "\| Origin \| **Externally
  generated, commissioned by the owner** \|" (also in PROVENANCE_e.md and PROVENANCE_f.md);
- the 2026-07-25 externally enhanced `scene_character.png` and `scene_car.png`, which are
  also not "produced in-house from vector masters".

A fix that edits only the two cited notes, or only line 19, leaves the same false statement
standing eighty lines away. That is the actionable part of this verdict.

### S2-C025, CONFIRMED, HIGH

This is the one I would leave at HIGH, and the decisive line is stronger than the finding
knew. `max_win_hold_gate.mjs:309-310`:

```
const readout = (page, id) =>
  page.locator(`[data-testid="${id}"]`).first().innerText().catch(() => '<absent>')
```

The catch converts a missing readout into the string `'<absent>'`, so `ok(win0 === win1, ...)`
at :467 and `ok(balance0 === balance1, ...)` at :466 both compare `'<absent>'` to `'<absent>'`
and pass. This is a WIRED CI gate ("browser: max-win hold") whose green cannot distinguish a
correctly held win figure from a HUD that never mounted. The layout gate's `if (!el) continue`
has the same property. I also checked the third candidate: `win_countup_steady_gate.mjs` never
touches `[data-testid="hud-win"]` at all, it builds a synthetic probe (`:94-106`,
`host.className = 'probe-host'` ... `s.textContent = c`) and measures character geometry, so
the finding's enumeration of hud-win consumers is complete for wired gates (the other eight
hits are unwired proofs and audits).

HIGH stands because REQ-068 is the win-presentation requirement, the two assertions that
appear to cover it are structurally unfailable in the exact form that matters, and this is the
fourth-time-repeated failure class named in convention (p): a green gate over a defect.

(`replay_blocker_proof.mjs:101-105`, "the EXACT form that shipped"). The single true residual
is that the script is not in `checks.yml`, and per the verification rules an unwired gate does
not refute a no-proof finding, so the cluster is not dismissed. But the honest tier for
