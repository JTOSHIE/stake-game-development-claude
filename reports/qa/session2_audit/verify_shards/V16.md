## PER CLUSTER REASONING

**S2-C095 (REQ-006, no child or child-like characters, no art appealing to minors).** The
quoted text is verbatim at `design-system/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md:57-58` and there is no gate: no
step in `.github/workflows/checks.yml` mentions underage, minors or branding, and none of
the 20 test files touches it. So the NO_PROOF half stands. **The evidence half does not.**
The claim asserts "an adult-proportioned helmeted racer figure", but the cited design-system
lines say only "the racer plus hover car, the game's identity character": nothing about
proportions. I opened the shipped asset. `SceneGroup.svelte:71` renders
`frontend/public/assets/themes/future-spinner/ui/scene_character.png`, 680x1344 confirmed by
`sips`, described in its own header comment at `SceneGroup.svelte:12-17` as "the pilot as a
FEATURE HERO ... scaled up so he reads as a presented feature" with "the antenna-tip blink".
Read off the rendered image, the helmet dome runs about y=118 to y=430 (about 312px) against
a figure of about y=118 to y=1285 (about 1167px), which is roughly **3.7 head heights**, and
the legs are about 405px, roughly **35 per cent** of figure height. Adult figure canon is
7 to 8 head heights with legs near 50 per cent; 4 to 5 heads is a toddler and 2 to 4 is
chibi or mascot. Allowing generously that a helmet is larger than the head inside it still
lands near 4.4. Add a bobble-tipped antenna. These are pixel estimates by eye, stated as
such, and the judgement is the owner's, not mine. But the finding's basis, that the surface
is manifestly adult-proportioned with "no toy or juvenile motif", is contradicted by the
artefact it is a claim about. **The real cause of the defect is not the missing gate: it is
that the compliance position on REQ-006 has never been measured against the shipped asset at
all, in either direction.** Escalate for an owner ruling before submission, since the
identity character is not a cheap late change.

**S2-C096 (REQ-016, the template disclaimer's closing line).** The `PaytableModal.svelte:62-64`
quote is verbatim, and the grep result is honest: no ruling exists in `REVIEW_TRACKER.md`,
`QUALITY_CHARTER.md` or `COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md`. **But the obligation is not undetermined, and
the authority settles it in one sentence on the page the register cites.**
`approval_guidelines_general_disclaimer.md:20` reads "You are able to use our template
disclaimer, or your own, so long as the same message is clearly conveyed", and the "same
message" is defined two lines earlier as being about game operation and RGS-dictated payouts,
not about the template's own attribution footer. `TM and © 2026 Stake Engine.` is the closing
line **of an optional template**, so replacing it with our own notice needs no ruling, and
doing so is what this project's own no-Stake-branding rule requires anyway. A ruling would be
tidy; its absence is not a compliance gap. Refuted, and severity drops to LOW. The residual
worth noting is in the new findings below.

**S2-C098 (REQ-151, submit only a finished build).** The quotes are verbatim at
`scripts/kit_build.mjs:3` and `:90-96`, and I confirmed zero hits for `kit_build` anywhere
under `.github/`. Note in passing that the claim greps "package.json" at the repository root,
which does not exist; the only one is `frontend/package.json`. **The diagnosis is unsound and
its implied remedy would produce exactly the failure convention (p) exists to prevent.** The
refusals are assertions about the state of a working machine at kit time: HEAD not pushed,
`frontend/` dirty. In CI, HEAD is always on the remote and the tree is always clean by
construction, so a `kit_build --check` step would be **incapable of going red on the condition
it exists to catch**: a gate that prints PASS. The refusals belong where they are, at the point
of action, and they already have the proof form this project asks for: `selfTest()` at
`kit_build.mjs:167`, with seeded cases including "seeded: the section that is superseded is NOT
chosen" at `:195`. The genuine residual is narrow and LOW: nothing runs that self-test
automatically, so a regression in the refusals would be found only by a human running
`--self-test`.

**S2-C099 (REQ-156, average at least 1 star over three reviewers).** I checked the register's
statement of the requirement against the source and it is accurate:
`approval_guidelines_submission_checklist.md:34-35` reads "Average ≥ 1 star → Game is approved
for production. / Average < 1 star → Game is rejected." **The finding is self-undermining.** A
star average is an output of three third-party reviewers; no artefact in this repository could
ever prove it, and demanding one is not a proof path. Worse, the path the finding cites IS the
defence it says does not exist: `STAKE_GUIDELINES_SELF_ASSESSMENT.md` is a 58-item pass over
the platform's own reviewer checklist, "captured and transcribed by the owner on 2026-07-26",
each row carrying "the evidence a reviewer could check, per (l.3)". Refuted, severity LOW.
**However the exact lines cited, 160 to 170, contain a real and different defect**, recorded
in the new findings below; the finding was looking at it and did not see it.

**S2-C100 (REQ-137, no Prohibited Content).** Both lines are verbatim at
`COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md:21-22`, and there is genuinely no gate: `grep -ni "trademark\|brand"
.github/workflows/checks.yml` returns nothing, and the only branding-adjacent script,
`frontend/scripts/trademark_variant_scan_au.mjs`, states at its line 11 "This script only
gathers data. It makes no clearance/similarity judgment." So a bare "verified" with no cited
evidence is the whole proof, which the register's own preamble forbids counting: a project
claim "must never be counted as requirements: doing so would let the project's own assertion
that it complies become evidence that it complies". **The defect is real; the attribution is
wrong.** REQ-137 is bound by R9-06's definition of Prohibited Content, which is pornographic
or lewd or obscene or unlawful, defamatory or discriminatory or hate speech, third-party IP
infringement, and incitement to racism. **"No underage appeal" is not in that definition at
all** (it belongs to REQ-006, `approval_guidelines.md:28`), and "No Stake branding" touches
only limb (c). So testing REQ-137 against these two lines answers one quarter of one limb, and
the honest finding is the wider one: **no project document audits shipped strings, symbols,
sounds and marketing art against the four Prohibited Content categories**, while the one limb
with real evidence, IP infringement, has it in a place the finding never looked
(`docs/records/trademark/2026-07-18/`). MEDIUM is right; the row should be re-pointed.

**S2-C101 (REQ-178, immediate written notice of Errors).** The platform text is verbatim at
`terms.md:293` and the greps hold: my own sweep over `docs`, `reports`, `COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md`
and `SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md`, excluding the mirror, returned only the register and its own
shards. So nothing in the project records the obligation. **NOT_MET is the wrong status.** This
is a continuing operational duty under a signed agreement, triggered by detecting an Error in a
live service; it cannot be breached before there is an agreement or an Error, so it is not
capable of being MET or NOT_MET today. The correct status is PARKED with a procedure to be
recorded, and `COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md:32-35`, "## Process reminders", is exactly the right home
for one line naming who notifies Carrot, in what form, and how fast. Real gap, cheap fix,
wrong tier: LOW.

**S2-C102 (REQ-179, submission is an upload for assessment).** Every quote checks out: the
dossier heading is at :228 rather than :229 and the numbered steps run :230-234, and the
approval-guidelines summary is verbatim at :9-10. The `kit_build` grep is correct. **The
finding does not stand as a defect.** REQ-179 restates the definition of submission, an upload
assessed against criteria Carrot sets and may change. It imposes no testable obligation on the
build, so there is nothing a proof path could assert, and the register's own scheme excludes
rows with "no obligation on the build". Meanwhile the project has done the only thing this row
could ask for: `SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md` section 5b documents the upload procedure step by step
against the named portal entry. Refuted, INFO at most. The kit_build-is-not-a-gate observation
is the same one as S2-C098 and carries the same answer there.
