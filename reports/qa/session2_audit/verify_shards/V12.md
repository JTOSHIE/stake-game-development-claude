## PER CLUSTER REASONING

### S2-C038 to S2-C043 (the six disclaimer sentences), CONFIRMED, severity too high

The observation is true and I could not break it. All six sentences are present verbatim in
one string at `prose.ts:118`, consumed at `PaytableModal.svelte:62` (`$: disclaimerText =
$tr('disclaimerBody')`) and rendered at `:406-409` under the comment `<!-- Disclaimer (Stake
Engine seven-point requirement) -->`. I attacked the no-proof claim with four candidate
refutations and all four failed:

1. **`locale_completeness_check.mjs`, wired at `checks.yml:223-225`.** This looked like the
   strongest candidate, since it fails a locale that drops a key. It reads
   `src/lib/i18n/translations.ts` (`:24`) and derives its required list from
   `export interface Translations` (`:29-37`). `disclaimerBody` does not live in that file at
   all: `grep` for it across `frontend/` returns only `prose.ts` (`:49`, `:118`, `:211`),
   `prose.locales.ts` and `PaytableModal.svelte:62`. **So the wired locale gate cannot see any
   of the disclaimer, which makes the finding stronger than as filed, not weaker.**
2. **`locale_prose_conformance.mjs`.** It is the only `frontend/scripts` file that mentions
   the disclaimer, and only in comments at `:18` and `:286`, exactly as claimed. It is also
   **not wired**: `grep -rn "locale_prose_conformance" .github/ package.json
   frontend/package.json` returns nothing. SCRIPT_UNWIRED, so it could not refute even if it
   asserted something.
3. **`vocabulary.test.ts`, wired at `checks.yml:246-248`.** It contains no occurrence of
   `disclaimer` in any form. Its `RULE_KEYS` list (`:116`) covers the six rules bullets, not
   the disclaimer paragraph.
4. **Committed captures.** `reports/qa/compliance_register/walk_shards/W07.md:25` cites
   `reports/screens/rules-paytable-2026-07-25/09-disclaimer.png` plus ten viewport and locale
   siblings for REQ-008. These are real and they do show the paragraph rendered. They do not
   refute a no-proof finding, because a PNG committed on 2026-07-28 cannot go red when a
   string is edited on 2026-07-30. That is a record of state, not a proof that fails.

**The one proof path the finding's search missed, stated so the record is accurate.**
`prose.ts:72` is `export type ProseStrings = Record<ProseKey, string>` and
`prose.locales.ts:33` is `export const proseLocales: Record<Exclude<Locale, 'en'>,
ProseStrings> = {`, so **`tsc` requires `disclaimerBody` in all fifteen non-English locales**,
and `npm run check` (svelte-check AND tsc) is wired at `checks.yml:432-434`. Deleting the KEY
therefore goes red. Deleting or rewording a SENTENCE inside the value does not, and that is
what these six requirements are about, so the claim survives. The finding would be better
worded as "key presence is type-enforced, sentence content is unguarded".

**Why HIGH is wrong.** All six sentences ship, in sixteen locales, are player reachable
through an always-enabled info button, and `W07.md:212` records the platform's own wording as
`identical`. A player or a reviewer sees nothing wrong today; the exposure is a future silent
edit. Under this project's own tiering that is a LOW row individually. I would grade the six
together as one MEDIUM class row rather than six HIGH ones, because six HIGH rows on a
satisfied requirement misdirect the fix order away from C044 and C045, which are real.

**One thing worth attaching to C038 rather than raising fresh.** The malfunction clause exists
in four wordings across two modes (`prose.ts:114` `Malfunctions void all pays and plays.`,
`:118` `Malfunction voids all wins and plays.`, `:197` `Malfunctions void all wins and
plays.`, `:211` `Malfunction voids all prizes and plays.`), and the social branch at `:197`
keeps the word `wins` that the social swap exists to remove. REQ-010 is still conveyed, so
this does not change C038's verdict. **It is already recorded** at
`reports/qa/stream_test/shards/STV.md:20-23` and `STT-MOBILES-2.md:76-83`, so it is not a new
finding and I have not claimed it as one.
