### S2-C083 CONFIRMED, severity reduced to MEDIUM

The observation stands and is stronger than the finding argued. The RTP band is on the
2026-07-04 mirror (`docs/stake-engine-live/math-verification.md:18`), on the 2026-07-25
mirror (`:28`) and on today's authority capture
(`docs/stake-engine-live/2026-07-29/approval_guidelines_math_verification.md:30`). The
outcome cap is a published-page line too:
`docs/stake-engine-live/2026-07-25/math-verification.md:20` and the 2026-07-29 capture at
`:22` both read "No game mode can contain more than 10,000,000 events". So the dossier's
"not yet on the published approval-guidelines page" is false for both limits, and section
9e (`SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md:770-781`) has seven correction rows and none of them touches
§8c, so the reader takes 8c as current.

**Where the finding overstates itself, and this is why claim_true is PARTLY.** It says
both limits "were mirrored into this repository three weeks before the announcement". Only
the RTP band was: `COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md:301` explicitly calls the events cap "**File size
restrictions, new since the 2026-07-04 capture**", so the cap reached the repo on
2026-07-25, the same day as the announcement, not three weeks before. The dossier's
sentence is still wrong, but it is wrong by one day for the cap, not by three weeks.

Severity is TOO_HIGH. Nothing here is player-visible, and the error runs in the safe
direction: it under-claims our own evidence rather than over-claiming compliance, and we
sit inside both limits on every reading (96.35% against 96.70%, 100,000 outcomes against
10,000,000). What it costs is a reviewer's confidence that we have read their page.
MEDIUM.
