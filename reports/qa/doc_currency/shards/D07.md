# D07: design-system provenance, document currency

Scope: the 17 markdown documents under `design-system/brand/` (every GENERATION_NOTE,
PROVENANCE, derivation and delivery record), tested against the tree at HEAD. Every
recorded source hash, shipped hash and dimension figure in the sample was recomputed from
the actual committed file with `shasum -a 256`, `stat -f%z` and `sips`; every named path was
stat'd; every stated arithmetic derivation was recomputed against the constants it cites.
No project script was executed.

animation pipeline, provenance recorded and measured, alpha and anchors preserved, siblings
checked), the recorded-and-measured condition holds almost everywhere: 96 of 96 hash and
byte figures I recomputed matched. **The condition that fails is the fifth, siblings**, and
it fails exactly where CLAUDE.md warned it would: "nothing in the build will say so".

## NOTES

**What the sample actually shows.** The hash discipline in this scope is the strongest I
found: 96 of 96 recorded hash and byte figures recomputed identically, including two
external source files still sitting on the owner's Desktop and Downloads with the exact
hashes recorded months of work later, and a 25-file supplier pack hashed row by row. The
arithmetic is also sound: five separate derivation ratios, two perimeter and area figures,
a percentage and a KB sum all recompute. **The failures are not measurement failures. They
are all "the world moved and the note did not"**, which is a different defect with a
different fix.

**D07-01 is the one a reader would act on wrongly**, and I want to be precise about its
limits. What is established: the tile background master and the submission asset
`FutureSpinner-BG.jpg` are byte-identical to each other and both predate the 2026-07-27
replacement of `bg_base.jpg` by an externally commissioned new design measured at Pearson r
0.3850 against what it replaced. What is NOT established by me: what the two images look
like side by side. I did not open them and I am not claiming a reviewer would see two
different cities. The finding is that the note's stated basis for continuity is gone, and
that CLAUDE.md's fifth external-asset condition ("Check what else is derived from it. An
adopted asset with siblings computed from the old one leaves the set incoherent, and nothing
in the build will say so") is the exact test that would have caught it. The adopting session
did derive the Overdrive sibling, and recorded doing so; the tile and delivery siblings were
not in view.
