## NOTES

**Why C08-01 and C08-02 are HIGH and not a typo.** Seven plus five is twelve, and the dossier
knows: it corrected this exact count on 2026-07-26, wrote out the reasoning at :275 to :282,
and then the 2026-07-28 rewording that introduced the two-set table reintroduced the wrong
number in the new table while leaving the correction paragraph two lines below it. The two
statements are eight lines apart in the same section. The harm is stated by the document
itself, in the sentence explaining why the earlier correction mattered, and it lands on a
first-time uploader following the dossier at the moment of submission. `BOOKS_MANIFEST.md:32`
carries the same eleven, so two committed documents in this scope agree with each other and
disagree with the hash table that both point at.

**C08-03 is the finding I most want a hostile read of, and here is the case against it.** The
workflow is `workflow_dispatch` only, `do_upload` defaults false, and C08-05's placeholder team
slug means a real run would fail at stakecli anyway. So the defect is latent, not live, and
nobody has been burned by it. What makes it worth a HIGH anyway is that it is a SILENT
incompleteness on the submission path: the workflow's own validation step is written to catch
exactly this class ("Validate bundles exist") and passes, because it tests `index.json` and
`index.json` is the one file that survives the checkout. A future session that fills in the
team slug and flips `do_upload` gets a green run and an incomplete math bundle. I checked the
alternative reading, that the books might be tracked via LFS or force-added like the lookup
tables: they are not, `git ls-files --error-unmatch` fails on `books_base.jsonl.zst`, and
`BOOKS_MANIFEST.md` plus dossier 5c both rule that they never will be, with GitHub's 100 MB
limit making two of them impossible regardless.

**C08-11 is a finding against the census instrument, so I tested it rather than inferred it.**
The brief's own warning is that a search returning nothing may be incapable of returning
something. The candidate list contains no markdown at all, which has two possible causes:
every `.md` in the tree is referenced, or `.md` cannot be a candidate. I falsified the first by
picking eight files in my own scope and grepping the whole tree for each basename: zero hits
for all eight, and none of the eight is in the list. So the second holds. The consequence for
the marshal is bounded and specific: the 550 is a floor across seven extensions, and the
markdown estate (255 files under `docs/`, 21 at root, the whole of `reports/`) has not been
assessed for orphanhood by this instrument at all. That does not mean the markdown is dead. It
means nobody has looked, and the census should not be read as though somebody had.

**Why I did not report `audit/` as dead weight despite C08-12.** Zero inbound references plus
demonstrably stale contents plus a FAIL headline reads like a directory that should go, and I
nearly wrote that. It does not survive the test. `SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md:354` still names
`audit/rgs_endpoint_test.py` as a live pre-submission step, `scripts/validate_math.py` and
`scripts/review_events_stateless_scan.py` sit beside the same evidence chain at dossier :318,
and the archived prompts cite `audit/analyze.py` as the reference method for RTP arithmetic. So
the directory is load-bearing for method and for one outstanding step, while its 22 June
reports are superseded narrative. The fix that follows from the evidence is a dated
supersession banner on the eight reports and the three JSONs, of the exact form
`HANDOVER_2026-07-25_Fable.md:1` already uses, not a deletion. I have not proposed the wording;
that is an owner call, and the maths figures inside are (l.8) territory.

**The mechanical class column, again, because it will look like a gap.** I could have back-fitted
the path rules from the counts (1556 evidence, 1249 skin, 534 records, 173 tooling, 91 engine)
and asserted a class per file. That is guessing at an instrument's internals in order to grade
it, and it would have produced 63 confident rows of which some fraction would be wrong in a way
nobody downstream could check. `not itemised` is the honest entry. Where the class genuinely
matters I stated `true_class` from the opened file, and the three UNCLASSIFIED rows (C08-15 to
C08-17) are the only ones where the mechanical class is quotable, so they are the only ones
filed as MISCLASSIFIED.

**One item I could not settle, recorded as UNKNOWN rather than filled.** Whether the `audit/`
reports' FAIL verdicts were subsequently closed. `FS_PopoutS_Verification.md` fails Popout S
and the mobile widths; establishing whether that is still true needs the current layout gates
and a rendered surface, which is another squad's instrument and not mine. I have reported the
missing supersession marker, which is checkable from my scope, and left the verdict alone.
