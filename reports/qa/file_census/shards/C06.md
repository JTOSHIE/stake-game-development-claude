## NOTES

**The mechanical_class column in this shard is an inference, and that is the census's
problem rather than mine.** `CENSUS_MECHANICAL.md` publishes five totals, a list of 27
UNCLASSIFIED files and a per-directory orphan table. It does not publish the per-file
classification, and `git log --oneline -3 -- reports/qa/file_census/` returns nothing, so
the artefact is untracked and its generator is not in the tree. I could not open the
generator, so I could not verify the rule that classified any file in my scope. What I DID
verify is that the orphan table is internally consistent with the candidate list for my
scope: the table's `docs/reference` 19, `docs/assets` 9, `docs/fe_assets` 3 and
`docs/records` 3 sum to 34, and grepping `^docs/` out of `orphan_candidates.txt` while
excluding `stake-engine-live` returns exactly 34 lines. On that basis I have written
`records` for every `docs/` file, since none of the 27 UNCLASSIFIED is under `docs/` and
`records` is the only class whose description ("decision records, method documents,
verbatim briefs, doc captures") covers the tree. If that inference is wrong, C06-01, C06-04
and C06-05 are wrong with it, and the marshal should discount them rather than the
underlying observations, which stand on their own quotes.

**The three track manifests are the densest thing in this scope and they carry three
separate defects, which is why they get three rows.** They are machine-read CI config
misfiled as prose (C06-01); they contradict each other about a glob (C06-02); and they are
scoped to branches that were deleted a day before this audit (C06-03). The contradiction is
the one worth acting on. `quality-sweep.manifest` records that the integrator narrowed
`reports/qa/**` to `reports/qa/*` on 2026-07-26 to fix collision SA-013.
`screenshot-analyst.manifest` still carries a 14-line comment block asserting the collision
is live and unresolvable from inside its track. Both files are committed on `main` today.
A reader who opens the second one first will conclude CI is broken; a reader who opens the
first one will conclude it is fine. Both readings are supported by the repository. That is
the shape the brief asked for: two files in scope asserting incompatible things.

**On the branch staleness, and why I did not stop at the manifests.** The
`collect-prototype` subtlety in `CLAUDE.md` is explicit that a branch with zero unique
commits is not automatically deletable, so I checked the deletion rather than assuming it:
`BRANCH_HYGIENE_2026-07-28.md` rows 22 to 24 record all three tracks as deleted with tip
SHAs, and `git branch -r` independently returns six branches with no `track/*` among them.
Two sources, and they do not share an input: one is a committed record, the other is the
remote's own ref list. The consequence is that `docs/records/tracks/` holds three live CI
inputs and two briefs describing a working mode that no branch currently uses, plus
`FINDINGS_LEDGER.md` declaring an owner that does not exist (C06-04).

**The favicon bundle is the one place I split an orphan judgement in two, and the split is
deliberate.** `android-chrome-192x192.png` and `-512x512.png` ARE literally referenced,
by `site.webmanifest` in the same directory, so listing them as zero-reference candidates is
a straight scan error and I recorded it as NOT_ORPHAN (C06-08). But the reference chain
terminates: `webmanifest` appears nowhere in the tree, so nothing loads the manifest that
loads them. Reporting each file individually would have produced two refutations and three
confirmations from one dead directory and told the marshal nothing useful, so C06-09 states
the set-level truth alongside them. The scan error is also informative in the other
direction, and it is the C06 instance of the lesson `CENSUS_MECHANICAL.md` records at its
own line 92. `docs/assets/favicon.ico` and `docs/assets/favicon_io/favicon.ico` are ABSENT
from the candidate list, but the only `favicon.ico` matches in the tree are
`we_roll_spinners_favicon.ico` in `design-system/brand/provider_mark/`. The scan matched a
SUBSTRING of a different filename and cleared two files it should have flagged. So the
candidate list under-reports as well as over-reports, and "not on the list" is not evidence
of a reference any more than "on the list" is evidence of death.

**I caught my own instrument lying, and it is worth one paragraph because the brief's whole
premise is that instruments lie.** My first dash census over the scope returned ZERO hits
across all 104 `.md` and `.txt` files. I had already read `docs/HUD_SPEC.md:1` and seen an
em dash in it with my own eyes, so I tested the instrument against that known case:
`grep -c '—' docs/HUD_SPEC.md` returned 9. The multibyte alternation in my first pass was
silently matching nothing. Re-run with `LC_ALL=C` and explicit UTF-8 byte sequences, the
scope holds 307 em or en dashes across 16 files. Had I trusted the first run, this shard
would have reported the docs tree clean of dashes, which is the exact false-negative shape
that produced TR-060. Convention `FULL_AUDIT_METHOD.md` 2.6 asks for this test; it took one
command and it changed the answer completely.

project-authored prose that the 2026-07-26 purge should have swept and did not, which is the
