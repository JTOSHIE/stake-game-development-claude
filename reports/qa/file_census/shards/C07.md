**On C07-01, which is the only finding in this shard I would refuse to let a tier filter
drop.** My brief told me 550 orphan candidates existed and to judge the ones in my scope.
There are none, and the temptation is to report zero and move on, which is exactly the
shape of the two false findings this wave has already produced, only inverted: a search
returned nothing and the question is whether it could have returned something. It could.
I picked a PNG under `reports/screens/scatter-anticipation/`, grepped its basename over
the whole tree with binary files included as text, and got nothing back, while the control
grep for the containing directory name returned six files including `CLAUDE.md`. Under

in any tracked text file") that PNG is a candidate, and it is not on the list. PNGs are
demonstrably eligible, since the list's own first twenty lines are PNGs under
`design-system/brand`. The only reading left is that `reports/` was not in the scanned
universe. That is worth a HIGH because the marshal is being handed a 550-line list to
disposition and would otherwise conclude the evidence tree was scanned and clean, when
1559 files, 43 per cent of the tracked tree, were never looked at. C07-10 is my
substitute at directory granularity, and it is honestly coarser than a file-level scan.
