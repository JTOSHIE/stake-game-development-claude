## The verbatim platform text for each, so you never work from the paraphrase

### REQ-001

- source: `approval_guidelines.md:18`
- platform text, verbatim: "Our team will inspect the game for functionality, clarity, communication, and technical performance. These factors determine the suitability of your game for publication."
- what it requires: The shipped game must be functional, clear, communicative of what is happening, and technically performant, because these are the stated inspection axes.

### REQ-002

- source: `approval_guidelines.md:23`
- platform text, verbatim: "Games cannot include jackpots, gamble features, continuation, or early cashout options."
- what it requires: Ship no jackpot, no gamble/double-up, no continuation of a prior round, and no early cashout control.

### REQ-003

- source: `approval_guidelines.md:24`
- platform text, verbatim: "Team names, game titles, and assets must comply with intellectual property/copyright law. Infringement is grounds for rejection."
- what it requires: Every shipped asset and the game title must be clear of third-party IP; the team name carries the same obligation on the studio side.

### REQ-004

- source: `approval_guidelines.md:26`
- platform text, verbatim: "Game assets cannot include material with Stake™ branding or themes."
- what it requires: No Stake marks, logos or Stake-themed motifs anywhere in shipped art, audio or text.

### REQ-005

- source: `approval_guidelines.md:27`
- platform text, verbatim: "Approval is at the discretion of the reviewer. Games deemed offensive, explicit, in poor taste, or of insufficient quality may be rejected."
- what it requires: Content and finish must clear a subjective reviewer bar for taste and quality; there is no objective threshold to satisfy.

### REQ-006

- source: `approval_guidelines.md:28`
- platform text, verbatim: "Games that promote, encourage, or are likely to appeal to underage persons are not permitted. This includes artistic depictions of children or child-like characters in any gambling context."
- what it requires: No child or child-like characters and no art, theme or tone that would appeal to minors.

### REQ-007

- source: `approval_guidelines.md:29`
- platform text, verbatim: "Games will be automatically considered for publication on stake.us under the condition that they abide by strict language requirements (see Jurisdiction Requirements below)."
- what it requires: Meet the social language requirements, since stake.us consideration is automatic and conditional on them.

### REQ-008

- source: `approval_guidelines_general_disclaimer.md:18`
- platform text, verbatim: "The game rules/information popup must include a brief disclaimer regarding game operation."
- what it requires: The rules/info popup must carry a disclaimer about how the game operates.

### REQ-009

- source: `approval_guidelines_general_disclaimer.md:18`
- platform text, verbatim: "You are able to use our template disclaimer, or your own, so long as the same message is clearly conveyed."
- what it requires: Either reproduce the platform template disclaimer or write one that clearly conveys the same message; wording may differ, meaning may not.
