## The verbatim platform text for each, so you never work from the paraphrase

### REQ-188

- source: `terms.md:255`
- platform text, verbatim: "The Developer shall cooperate fully with Carrot and/or its Nominee in this regard, providing all necessary information, documentation, and technical assistance as reasonably requested by Carrot and/or its Nominee, at the Developer's cost where such requirements arise from the Game's design or Developer's obligations."
- what it requires: Fund and supply whatever the certification and jurisdictional-approval process asks for, where the requirement stems from our own design.

### REQ-189

- source: `terms.md:385`
- platform text, verbatim: "Carrot will notify the Developer and the Developer will take immediate corrective action, in good faith and in cooperation with Carrot and Stake (or relevant third-party provider), to rectify that issue as soon as reasonably practicable at no cost to Carrot."
- what it requires: On any notified breach of the clause 7.1a) game standards, remediate immediately at our cost; failure to satisfy Carrot leads to deactivation and licence termination under 7.1c).

### REQ-190

- source: `privacy.md:34+36-41`
- platform text, verbatim: "To provide a secure and personalized experience on Stake Engine, we collect and process specific categories of personal data: Your name Email address A link to your GitHub repository A brief description of how you plan to use Stake Engine Confirmation that you’re 18 years or older Confirmation of your status as either an individual developer or an authorized company representative" (JOINED from line 34 and lines 36 to 41, each line joined with a single space)
- what it requires: At registration the studio must supply these six items, including an explicit 18-or-over confirmation and a declaration of individual-developer or authorised-company-representative status, plus a link to the GitHub repository.

### REQ-191

- source: `giveaway_terms.md:40`
- platform text, verbatim: "To enter the Competition, an eligible applicant must before the Closing Date: (a) register for a Stake Engine account at https://stake-engine.com/signin (if you do not already hold one); (b) build and develop an original game using the Stake Engine Platform; and (c) submit your completed game via the Stake Engine Platform in accordance with Stake Engine's submission process and guidelines."
- what it requires: Three entry steps, all completed before the Closing Date: hold an account, build the game on the platform, and submit it through the platform's own submission process.

### REQ-192

- source: `giveaway_terms.md:25`
- platform text, verbatim: "Closing Date: 11:59pm AEDT on 1 August 2026."
- what it requires: Hard submission deadline for competition eligibility, 11:59pm AEDT 1 August 2026; entries after it are not considered, and the Promoter may move the date by publishing an update.

### REQ-193

- source: `giveaway_terms.md:42`
- platform text, verbatim: "To be eligible for the Prize, your game must achieve a minimum 3-star rating (3 Star Rating) which will be determined at Stake Engine's sole discretion upon consideration of (but not limited to): game quality, originality, playability, technical execution and commercial potential."
- what it requires: Prize eligibility requires the platform's own rating of at least 3 stars, awarded at Stake Engine's sole discretion across quality, originality, playability, technical execution and commercial potential; line 44 adds that each game must independently achieve it.

### REQ-194

- source: `payments.md:81`
- platform text, verbatim: "Add a wallet on your team settings — one wallet per team."
- what it requires: Register exactly one payout wallet on the team settings page; without it no payout can be made.
