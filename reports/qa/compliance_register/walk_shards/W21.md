## PER REQUIREMENT NOTES

### REQ-039 (mode cost in the rules matches the maths package) - SATISFIED

Opened `PaytableModal.svelte:299-346` as a block. The BET MODES page renders one card per
entry of `FS_MODES` and prints the cost twice: the multiplier `{m.cost}×` and the money
figure `{modePrice(m.cost)}`, where `modePrice` at `:151-152` is
`formatBalance(Math.round($betAmount * cost * CURRENCY_SCALE), $currencyCode || 'USD')`.
`FeatureMenu.svelte` prints the same field from the same array at `:372`, `:427` and `:480`
(line-scoped read). So the displayed cost is not a transcription of the maths cost, it IS
the same value, which removes the drift class rather than gating it.

Opened `fsModes.drift.test.ts` in full and `checks.yml` in full. The gate compares
`FS_MODES` against `games/future_spinner/library/publish_files/index.json` in BOTH
directions and is wired as gate 5 of the `static gates` job. The two sides are
independently generated (the index is emitted by the maths publish step), so this is real
corroboration rather than one file checked against itself.

**Weakness worth recording (convention (p)):** this gate ships NO seeded-violation
self-test. Every other gate family in `checks.yml` that claims a class closed runs
`--self-test` first (dash gate `:255-257`, machine tell `:285-287`, asset references
`:490-492`, and every browser leg). `fsModes.drift.test.ts` has never been seen to fail.
Its logic is short enough to read in one screen, which is why the verdict is still
SATISFIED, but the claim is unproven in the sense convention (p) means.

### REQ-040 (max win printed in the rules equals the maths max win, per mode) - NO_PROOF

The implementation is present and, read against the specification, correct:
`_WINCAP = 5000.0` at `game_config.py:52` (line-scoped read) and `FS_MAX_WIN_LABEL =
'5,000×'` at `fsModes.ts:139` (block read) agree, and the label is rendered on all five
mode cards plus the general rules row, so the figure is displayed per mode as required.

**The maths operand IS evidenced per mode, and the BINDING is not.** I opened
`scripts/qa/bet_level_compliance.py` in full: it reads the frozen publish tables, takes
`max_x = ordered[0][0]` at `:147` and writes it per mode, and its committed output
`reports/qa/bet_level_compliance_raw_2026-07-25.json` records `"max_x": "5000"` five times,
once for each mode, alongside `"rtp_pct"` values of 96.3499998 to 96.3499999. So the
platform's "for each mode" clause is answered on the maths side by a committed artefact I
opened rather than by an assumption. Graded SCRIPT_UNWIRED rather than EVIDENCE because the
script appears in no CI job (I read `checks.yml` end to end; its only python step is the
books-verifier self-test at `:510-511`), so the figure is a dated 2026-07-25 snapshot that a
sanctioned maths pass would leave stale, and because a wrong LABEL leaves this artefact
still correct: only the pair disagrees, and nothing compares the pair.

**Nothing defends the agreement itself.** I looked in four places. (1) `fsModes.drift.test.ts`:
its only maths-side input is `index.json`'s `{name, cost}` pairs, and I opened `index.json`
in full - it has no max-win field, so no drift test on max win is even possible from that
source. (2) The 20 test files: the only max-win assertions are
`replayRounds.test.ts:104` and `:141`, `check('pays exactly the cap',
round.payoutMultiplier, CENTIBET_CAP)`, which bind the shipped BOOKS to
`roundInterpreter.ts:23` `CENTIBET_CAP = 500_000`, not to the paytable literal.
(3) `frontend/scripts/max_win_hold_gate.mjs`, which is wired at `checks.yml:589-590`: its
subject is the overlay HOLD duration, and its `5,000x` mentions are comments and a
synthetic round payload, not an assertion about the printed rules figure.
(4) `paytable_card_fill_gate.mjs`, wired at `checks.yml:579-580`: its assertion is
geometric, `PAYTABLE CARD FILL GATE: PASS (every card fill encloses its frame, ...`, so it
would catch the figure being CLIPPED (which is TR-037's history) but not the figure being
WRONG.

So an edit of one character in `fsModes.ts:139`, or a sanctioned maths pass that changed
`_WINCAP`, ships a rules page that contradicts the maths and every check in the repository
stays green. That is the finding.

### REQ-041 (never submit a bet above 500,000 USD) - NO_PROOF

What I established. The frontend cannot invent a bet amount: `betLadder.ts:39-41` takes the
ladder from `rgsBetLevels` (the authenticate response) and falls back to `BET_LEVELS` only
when the platform supplied none, and the module's own header at `:102-120` states the
consequence, `it refuses anything that is not ON the active ladder ... Nothing here
synthesises an amount, rounds one, or interpolates between two levels`. `rgsService.ts`
(locked, read as text) sends `amount: amountMicros` with `mode: get(selectedBetMode)`, so
the submitted amount is a base-bet ladder level and the SERVER applies the cost multiplier,
consistent with `fsModes.ts:47` `Cost as a multiple of the base bet (server applies this)`.
On the fallback ladder the top level is 100.00, so even the 400x tier prices at 40,000, well
under the ceiling. `rgsService.ts:364` maps the platform's own code to a player message,
`ERR_VAL:         'Invalid bet amount. Please choose a valid bet and try again.'`.

What I could NOT establish, and what makes this a finding. No artefact in the repository
asserts the 500,000 ceiling, and no artefact bounds the BUY COST, which is the exposed
quantity: the platform's own limits table that I opened at `COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md:218-233`
distinguishes `Maximum Bet Cost | $100,000 | $500,000` (a cost, so multiplier included)
from the flat `$500,000 USD` bet-size rejection. On a platform ladder whose top level times
400 exceeds 500,000 - and the live captures cited at `REVIEW_TRACKER.md:308` show the
platform's own ledger printing costs as large as `XGC 500,000.00` - the NITRO OVERDRIVE card
would price, enable and submit a round that the RGS answers with 400. `betLadder.test.ts:80`
(line-scoped read) asserts `every guard refuses to act off ladder`, which is the adjacent
property and is wired as gate 8 at `checks.yml:297-299`, but adding a level above the
ceiling to `BET_LEVELS`, or shipping a 400x tier against a high-nominal ladder, would not
turn it red.

I opened the one candidate whose NAME promises this requirement,
`scripts/qa/bet_level_compliance.py`, in full. It does not check a bet ceiling. Its
docstring at `:20-30` enumerates what it produces, `RTP`, `SD`, `P(>=5000x)`,
`P(>=10000x)`, `ETL`, `CVaR`, and its subject is the platform's per-star-tier maths limits
table, not the flat bet-size rejection. So the most plausible-looking path is not one, which
is precisely the kind of citation this audit forbids being made on the filename.
