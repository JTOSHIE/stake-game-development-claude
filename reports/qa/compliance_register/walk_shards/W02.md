## PER REQUIREMENT NOTES

**REQ-061.** The paytable is complete on the shipped surface: eight paying symbols with all three paying combination lengths each (3, 4, 5 of a kind on 1,024 ways), WILD and SCAT carrying notes rather than pay rows because WILD has no independent pay and SCAT pays off the scatter table. The values match the CLAUDE.md true-game-facts line (`H1 22/6/1.5 down to L3 0.65/0.20/0.08 per way`). **What I could not establish is any gate defending it.** I grepped `frontend/scripts/`, `scripts/` and `tools/` for `fs-sym-card`, `SYMBOLS`, `symbolPayouts` and `fs-trig`: the only two hits outside the venv are `paytable_card_fill_gate.mjs` and `paytable_reskin_proof.mjs`. I read the card fill gate's own statement of what it asserts (`ENCLOSURE  the fill covers the frame's inner box` and `CONTAINMENT every text box and image inside a card lies inside that card's fill`, lines 31-36): it is a geometry gate. Deleting the `5×` row, or editing `22` to `2.2`, changes neither enclosure nor containment, so the wired paytable gate would stay green. There is no drift test between `PaytableModal.svelte`'s `SYMBOLS` and `games/future_spinner/game_config.py`, the way `fsModes.drift.test.ts` exists for the mode table. That gap is the finding here, not the code.

**REQ-062.** Scatter instant-pay values are fully enumerated (1x, 3x, 10x) in three places: the SCAT card note, the rules line, and the trigger table's Instant Award column. **The meter multiplier values are stated as a RULE rather than enumerated.** The key is not in `prose.ts` (that table carries `scatterSummary` and `rulesScatterMult` only); it is at `frontend/src/lib/i18n/translations.ts:1541`, `rulesOverdriveMeter: 'The Overdrive meter starts at 1× and rises +1× after every winning free spin, multiplying all later wins. It never resets during the feature.',` and I read the rendered form of it in frame 025. So the disclosure gives the start value and the increment and the persistence, from which the obtainable set is derivable, but there is no list. Whether the platform's "list all obtainable values" is satisfied by a derivation rule for an open-ended meter is a judgement call for the marshal or the owner, not mine: I record that the scatter half is enumerated and evidenced and the meter half is ruled rather than listed. Note also that the meter's ceiling is not stated anywhere on the surface I opened, so a player cannot compute the top of the range from the paytable alone without also knowing the retrigger behaviour. Nothing gates the meter disclosure either way.

**REQ-063.** Clean. The table is exactly the platform's own example shape (scatter count to spins awarded), and it additionally carries the instant award. Same proof gap as REQ-061: `TRIGGER_TABLE` is a hand-maintained literal with a comment saying it "matches CLAUDE.md true game facts exactly" and nothing mechanical checks that claim against `games/future_spinner/`.

**REQ-064.** Eight rows, each with a real captured icon (not a hand-drawn one), a name and a description, both routed through `$tr`. I checked whether the `data-testid="interface-guide"` hook is defended in CI: `grep -rln "interface-guide" frontend/scripts/` returns `regen_interface_guide_icons.mjs`, `interface_guide_icon_proof.mjs`, `mini_player_proof.mjs`, `owner_audit_v4_proof.mjs`, `social_dom_conformance.mjs`, `rules_conformance_proof.mjs`, `social_string_conformance.mjs`. **None of those seven appears in any `run:` line of `.github/workflows/checks.yml`** (I read every `run:` line in that file). So the guide's proof is the committed capture only. Note also KNOWN_OPEN's Q-16 park lists `Interface Guide` among hardcoded English paytable headers; at HEAD the heading is `{$tr('interfaceGuideHeading')}`, so that half of the park reads stale, but Q-16 is not mine to close.

**REQ-065 / REQ-066.** These two are the strongest pair in the slice. `bet_selector_gate.mjs` is wired at `checks.yml:594-596` and runs `--self-test` first, and its self-test plants the three real forms (`problems.push('SEED NOT CAUGHT: an unwired BET window')`, `'SEED NOT CAUGHT: setBetLevel without its off-ladder refusal'`, `'SEED NOT CAUGHT: a hardcoded ladder in the panel')`), so convention (p) is satisfied rather than assumed. The gate deliberately authenticates with an eight-level ladder "sharing NOT ONE VALUE with `BET_LEVELS`", which is what makes the "not hardcoded" assertion real. `betLadder.test.ts` is separately wired at `checks.yml:297-299` and reproduces the R5/TR-013 arithmetic. Severity STREAM only because the surface is player-visible; nothing is wrong here.

**REQ-067.** THIS IS THE FINDING. The implementation is present and correct in all four HUD layouts, and the balance is live (`$balance` from the locked `gameStore.ts`, written from the authenticate/play/end-round responses; `rgsService.contract.test.ts` is wired at `checks.yml:385-387` and asserts `check('balance.amount is read from the NESTED balance object, in micros', auth.balance, 123.45)` and `check('balance comes from the nested object', p.balance, 99)`, which defends the DATA path). What is undefended is the DISPLAY. The one artefact that reads the rendered readout is `currency_conformance.mjs:160` and its committed output `reports/qa/currency_conformance_2026-07-25.json`, and that script is not in `checks.yml`, so it is SCRIPT_UNWIRED. The wired `layout_fit_gate.mjs` reads the same `[data-testid="hud-balance"]` selector but its loop is `if (!el) continue`, so removing the balance pod makes the gate greener, not redder. `max_win_hold_gate.mjs` compares two reads for equality, which an empty string satisfies. I did not find a single wired assertion that the balance is present or that it moves with a wager. Related but distinct: KNOWN_OPEN records `C-20, the balance readout claim, refuted by both panel seats` as CLOSED, which was a rendering claim, not this proof gap, so I am not reporting this as KNOWN(C-20).
**The omission is declared rather than accidental, and that is worth recording exactly.** `.github/workflows/checks.yml:17-21` states the scope decision in the workflow's own words: `SCOPE, still deliberately narrow. The Playwright suites that need a real dev server and a real environment (qa_soak, portrait_layout_conformance, currency_conformance, the proof scripts) stay local and in the external audit: they take tens of minutes and their value is in a real environment rather than in a CI container.` So the balance readout is defended by a script the project has decided not to wire, plus one committed JSON from 2026-07-25. By this audit's grading that is still SCRIPT_UNWIRED: nothing goes red if the pod disappears. The cheap repair, if one is wanted, is a presence assertion in the already-wired `layout_fit_gate.mjs`, i.e. replacing `if (!el) continue` with a recorded absence, which costs no browser time because that gate already loads the HUD at seven presets.
**Also note the ordering hazard I did not chase:** the balance figure is one of the money readouts in TR-115 / TR-086 (money display fit failures), which is OPEN. That is a fit finding, not this one.

But the wired gates that touch `hud-win` are a clipping gate that skips absence and a hold gate that only compares two samples for equality. `win_countup_steady_gate.mjs` IS wired (`checks.yml:583-584`) with a self-test, and I read its stated assertions (`Every digit box renders the same width`; `Two amounts with the SAME character count but DIFFERENT digits render at the same total width`; `A digit-free control still renders`): it proves the amount does not shimmy, which presupposes an amount but does not assert that a non-zero payout produces one. Committed frames `012_desktop_win_presentation.png` and `015_desktop_bigwin_settled.png` exist and I list them as corroboration, but I graded the proof on the wired scripts, which is why this is NO_PROOF rather than SATISFIED-with-evidence.

**REQ-069.** The platform text asks for two things and both are met by different mechanisms, so I checked both. (1) INCREMENTAL UPDATE: `startWinCountUp` tweens `displayedWinAmount` from the previous figure to the new target on every increase, and during the feature the visible in-feature total is `FreeSpinsPresentation`'s `runningTotalCentibets`, sourced per spin from `roundInterpreter`, which is a genuine per-action tally rather than a cosmetic roll. Committed frames `013_desktop_transition_bigwin_countup_early.png` and `014_desktop_transition_bigwin_countup_late.png` capture the count-up mid-flight. (2) LANDS ON THE FINAL MULTIPLIER: this is the load-bearing half and it IS wired. `replayRounds.test.ts` runs in CI and asserts the presented wins sum to the book's own `payoutMultiplier` in integer centibets over committed fixture rounds. `roundInterpreter.test.ts` makes the stronger exact-total claim over the curated samples (`the sum of all presented win events, capped at the win cap, must equal the round payoutMultiplier EXACTLY (integer centibets, no float drift)`), but **it is NOT wired**: `grep -n "roundInterpreter" .github/workflows/checks.yml` returns nothing, so `roundInterpreter.test.ts` and `roundInterpreter.determinism.test.ts` are two of the twenty test files that no CI step runs. I graded CI_GATE on the wired `replayRounds.test.ts` and record the unwired stronger test as a separate weakness.
