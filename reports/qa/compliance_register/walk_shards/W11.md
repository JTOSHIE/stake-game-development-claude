## PER REQUIREMENT NOTES

### REQ-037 (SATISFIED, with a named narrowness)

The phrase is in the module's verbatim transcription of the platform table at
`vocabulary.ts:78`, and `sv()` rewrites it in social mode; `SUBSTITUTIONS` is sorted longest
first (`vocabulary.ts:104`, `out.sort((a, b) => b.phrase.length - a.phrase.length)`) so
`place your bets` wins over `bets` and cannot be shortened into a different platform row.
I also grepped `frontend/src`, `frontend/scripts` and `scripts/qa` for the phrase: the only
occurrence in the tree is the table row itself, so nothing renders it today.

The proof is real and CI wired, and it is narrower than it looks, which is worth recording:
`vocabulary.test.ts:128-132` runs `scanProhibited` over 22 resolved locale keys (six rules
lines, sixteen guide fields) in English social, not over all rendered text. The full DOM
sweep that would cover the rest, `frontend/scripts/social_dom_conformance.mjs`, is NOT in
`checks.yml` (only named in a comment at `checks.yml:245`), so it is SCRIPT_UNWIRED and its
value is the dated committed JSON rather than a gate. The sv() cases in the test do not
exercise `place your bets` itself; what defends this specific row is the mirror-equality
assertion at `vocabulary.test.ts:56`. Convention (p): I found no seeded-violation self test
for gate 6b, so its PASS is unproven in the sense (p) means.

### REQ-038 (NO_PROOF, STREAM)

Implementation is real and is NOT the vocabulary layer. `vocabulary.ts:120` deliberately
excludes the term: `currency: 'appears only in code identifiers, never player-visible',`
and `NEVER_REWRITE` is built from that map at `:123`. **That stated reason is factually
wrong at HEAD.** `ReplayMode.svelte:346` renders the word to a player, and the component
knows it: its own comment at `:343-344` says `The word "currency" is itself on the stake.us
prohibited-terms table (currency -> token), so the label switches in social mode.` The
component handles the swap by hand, correctly, so the requirement is met on that surface.

What is missing is any defence of it. Three places I looked and what each says:

- `vocabulary.test.ts` cannot catch it: `scanProhibited` skips never-rewrite terms unless
  asked (`vocabulary.ts:183`), and the test's social sweep calls it with no options.
- `frontend/scripts/a11y_social_terms_check.mjs:37-43` lists its `RESTRICTED` array
  verbatim, and `currency` is not in it (nor are `place your bets`, `bet/s` or `fund`).
- The committed DOM evidence, `reports/qa/social_dom_conformance_2026-07-25.json`, lists
  `currency` under `termsReportedNotFailed` and its `surfacesCovered` are `first-paint,
  features-menu, paytable, autoplay, session, spin, win-banner`. **Replay is not among
  them.** So the one surface that renders the word has never been scanned in social mode.

A one-character edit to `ReplayMode.svelte:346` would ship `Currency` into a social replay
session and nothing in this repository would go red. Grepped for `'Token'` and
`currency-display` across `frontend/scripts`, `scripts/qa`, `frontend/src` and `reports/qa`:
the only hits are the component itself and another walk shard. Not a match for any
`KNOWN_OPEN.md` row (replay is named there only by TR-114, the ghost pod, which is a
different defect).
