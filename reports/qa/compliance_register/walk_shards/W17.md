## PER REQUIREMENT NOTES

### REQ-109 (zero minor units per currency)

The platform table at `approval_guidelines_rgs_communication.md:54,60,61,66,68` was opened and
its rows read verbatim: `Japanese Yen JPY ¥ ¥10`, `Indonesian Rupiah IDR Rp Rp10`,
`South Korean Won KRW ₩ ₩10`, `Vietnamese Đồng VND ₫ 10 ₫`, `Chilean Peso CLP CLP 10 CLP`. The
formatter's set at `currency.ts:42` is exactly those five codes and no others, and both the
virtual and fiat branches take their decimal count from it rather than from a global 2. The
assertion at `currency.test.ts:81-84` is load-bearing rather than decorative: removing `JPY`
from `ZERO_DECIMAL` makes `formatBalance(1250 * S, 'JPY', 'en')` return `¥1,250.00`, the regex
`/\.\d/` then matches, and the run exits non-zero at `currency.test.ts:208`
(`process.exit(1)`). The step is in the `static gates` job of `checks.yml`, so it is a wired
gate.

One weakness worth recording under convention (p): this is a plain assertion file, not a gate
with a seeded-violation self-test, so nobody has watched it go red for THIS class. The
assertions are direct calls with literal expectations, which is the shape least likely to be
vacuous, but the (p) claim is unproven for it. A second weakness I could not close: the
platform's own Example column puts the VND and CLP symbols AFTER the amount (`10 ₫`,
`10 CLP`), and `currency.ts:214-220` delegates fiat placement wholly to `Intl` with
`currencyDisplay: 'narrowSymbol'`, so placement for those two is whatever the runtime ICU says
rather than what the platform table shows. No assertion anywhere pins fiat placement against
the platform table, and I did not run anything to find out what ICU actually emits. That is
adjacent to my slice rather than in it (my rows are the decimal count), so I flag it here
rather than opening a verdict on it.

### REQ-164 (3MB combined ceiling, background plus foreground)

The requirement IS met on the bytes: `stat` reports `FutureSpinner-BG.jpg` at 381,863 and
`FutureSpinner-FG.png` at 433,497, so 815,360 bytes against a 3,145,728 ceiling, and the
provider logo (40,314) is correctly excluded, matching the platform sentence which names only
the two. The finding is the proof path, not the code.

The only enforcement is `frontend/scripts/tile_delivery_build.mjs`, which does assert the
ceiling and exits non-zero. I searched `.github/workflows/checks.yml` for `tile`, `delivery`,
`design-system`, `brand`, `provider_mark` and `asset`: the only matches are the two
`asset_reference_gate.mjs` steps at `:490-496`, which resolve references inside `dist` and
never look at `design-system/brand/delivery/`. So the ceiling is checked by a script nobody
runs on a push. Swap in a larger background master and nothing in CI goes red; the committed
figure at `design-system/brand/delivery/README.md:14` ("796 KB against the 3 MB ceiling.
Within the limit.") would simply go stale, and it is captured output of that same script, so
it is not independent corroboration of it (convention (l.4)).

There is a second, structural weakness in the same place. `tile_delivery_build.mjs` WRITES
`design-system/brand/delivery/README.md` and re-copies all four delivered assets on every run
(`writeFileSync(join(DELIVERY, item.to), buf)` at line 114, `writeFileSync(join(DELIVERY,
'README.md'), ...)` at line 213). That is exactly the convention (h.1) shape already recorded
against `anticipation_proof.mjs`, `layout_fit_gate.mjs` and `contrast_gate.mjs`: a casual local
re-run dirties committed evidence. So this gate cannot be wired into CI as it stands without
either running it read-only or pointing its writes at a scratch path. I did not run it.

### REQ-165 and REQ-168 (what the background and foreground depict)

Both are `vis: YES` rows, so I judged them from the images rather than from prose. I opened
`design-system/brand/tile/tile_background_preview_1024.jpg` at 1024 wide and
`reports/screens/brand-tile-hero/tile_hero_full_on-dark.png` at 2000 wide; the defect class
here is "is this a scene or a logo/flat field" and "is there a recognisable hero subject",
both of which resolve comfortably at those sizes.

Caveat stated plainly: for REQ-165 I viewed the committed 1024 PREVIEW, not the 2048x1152
delivered master, because the preview is the committed proof frame. The preview's provenance
as a derivation of the master is asserted by its filename and by `TR-005`'s tracker row rather
than by anything I measured, so if the master and the preview ever diverged I would not have
seen it. For REQ-168 I viewed the `on-dark` proof sheet rather than the delivered PNG, for the
same reason: the alpha channel is only judgeable over a known field.

On REQ-168 the slice paraphrase says "a single representative character or hero item". The
platform text is `"A feature character or key item that represents the game"`, which does not
carry the word "single". The delivered foreground carries BOTH the pilot and the hover-car, in
the established in-game pose. I read that as satisfying the platform text as written, per
convention (l.7), and record the discrepancy so the marshal can rule differently if the
paraphrase was meant to bind.
