## PER REQUIREMENT NOTES

**REQ-188 (certification and jurisdictional-approval cooperation, at our cost).** The clause
obliges us to supply "all necessary information, documentation, and technical assistance as
reasonably requested" for certification and jurisdictional approval, at our cost where the
requirement comes from the game's design. The closest real implementation is the documentation
package that stands ready to be handed over: `SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md:22-37` inventories thirteen
artefacts including the PAR sheet ("\| 3 \| PAR sheet (five modes, FeatureMath v2 documented) \|
Overdrive pass + FeatureMath v2 \| MERGED to main \| Done \|") and the compliance evidence pack
quoted in the table, and `docs/records/compliance/STAKE_GUIDELINES_SELF_ASSESSMENT.md` carries
our position on all 58 guidelines. **That is a partial match and I am recording it as one**: it
is a supply of documentation, not a recorded acceptance of the cooperation-and-cost duty. What I
could not establish: any record of the cooperation obligation itself, of who owns it, or of the
cost allocation. `grep -rln "certification"` over all `*.md` outside `docs/stake-engine-live/`
returns only the audit report, a live-stats ingest note and this register's own three files;
`WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` section 1 (COMPANY REGISTER, 33 rows) has no certification row;
`.github/workflows/checks.yml` has no step touching `docs/`, the register, the dossier or the
checklist (I grepped it for `register|checklist|dossier|docs/` and the only hits are comments at
lines 158, 267 and 310); and `docs/records/reviews/REVIEW_TRACKER.md` has no row for it. One
caution on the impl quote itself: it asserts the maths is "CI-gated (scripts/validate_math.py)",
and that claim IS true, but not through `checks.yml`. It runs in a second workflow,
`.github/workflows/validate-math.yml:37-38`, "- name: Validate lookup tables against the
compliance rubric / run: python3 scripts/validate_math.py", path-filtered to `index.json`,
`lookUpTable_*.csv`, the script and the workflow file. So the gate is real and wired, and a
reader checking `checks.yml` alone would wrongly conclude the dossier was stale.

**REQ-189 (immediate corrective action on a notified breach, at no cost to Carrot).** I looked
for a recorded route by which a Carrot defect notice reaches this project and gets actioned:
`WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` sections 1 and 2, `OWNER_CHECKLIST.md` end to end, `SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md`
sections 1, 2 and 5, and greps for `corrective action`, `remediat` and `notify Carrot` across
those files. Nothing. What is there instead is the post-approval lockdown quoted in the table,
and it is worth flagging the tension rather than resolving it: a notified breach of the clause
7.1a) standards could require a maths or gameplay change, which the lockdown line says never
happens after approval, so the route for that case is defined in neither document. The nearest
thing to a process is the pre-submission machinery (the standing mandate in `CLAUDE.md` and
`REVIEW_TRACKER.md`), which is about defects we find, not defects Carrot notifies. I could not
establish any owner, SLA or escalation path, so I graded this NOT_MET rather than NO_PROOF: the
surface where it would live was opened and the obligation is absent from it. Severity MEDIUM
because the platform's stated remedy for failing to satisfy Carrot is deactivation and licence
termination, but nothing here is player-visible and no notice is outstanding.

**REQ-190 (six registration items, including 18-plus and individual-or-company-representative).**
`WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md:27` establishes that an account exists and that the developer ToS was
accepted at its creation, and `CLAUDE_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS_v7.md:3` records the GitHub link item:
"Repo: https://github.com/JTOSHIE/stake-game-development-claude". None of the other four items
(name, email, use description, the 18-plus confirmation, the developer-status declaration) is
recorded anywhere I could find. **That absence is largely correct by design and the register says
so at `WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md:6-10`**: "This repository is PUBLIC. This register carries **facts
only**. No personal document, no residential or business address, no ABN, no account number and
**no wallet address** is ever committed here or anywhere else in the repository ... Where a value
exists but must not be published, the register records **that it exists and where it is held**,
and nothing more." So in-repo proof of the six items cannot exist, and the correct proof would be
a register row saying the registration items are held off-repo, which is exactly what the rule's
own pattern asks for and what is missing. Two loose ends I can name: the row's own "Record date if
known" is still unfilled after the account was created, and nothing records which of the two
statuses (individual developer or authorised company representative) was declared, which now
matters because ASIC registration of We Roll Spinners landed on 2026-07-27
(`WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md:21`) and the platform requires the publisher name to match. Graded
NO_PROOF, LOW.

**REQ-193 (minimum 3-star rating, at Stake Engine's sole discretion).** Implementation: the target
is recorded, at `WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md:36` (quoted in the table) and again at
`CLAUDE_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS_v7.md:3`, "Quality target: 3 stars". The second half of the

guidelines prior to completing them on stake engine\"*. So this is our internal pass, run before
the owner ticks anything. Each row carries the evidence a reviewer could check, per (l.3)." Its
status vocabulary is strict enough to be usable as evidence (PASS means "Verified from code, a
test, or a capture. Evidence cited."), and it does not hide its gaps: row 26 reads "`HudOverlay.svelte:150
startAuto()` starts immediately when a count is chosen ... there is no explicit confirm dialog.
Needs a ruling on whether the menu counts." The only objectively checkable
component of the tier is the platform's automated bet-level verification limit set, and
`WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md:53` records our position on it: "Every determinable constraint passes on
**both** tiers, most by wide margins. Independently recomputed from the shipped tables in
`reports/qa/math_bet_level_compliance_2026-07-25.md`", with the proximity flag at line 57 kept
honestly ("worst-case ETL(>=40x cost) is 0.6654 (OVERBOOST), which is 73.93% of the 3-star limit
but **83.17% of the 2-star limit**"). I opened that report and it is real committed evidence: a
two-computer reconciliation against Fable's independent figures, exact rational arithmetic, source
tables named. **Why the verdict is UNKNOWN rather than SATISFIED**: the requirement is an award
made at a third party's sole discretion across five named criteria, no rating exists yet
(`STAKE_GUIDELINES_SELF_ASSESSMENT.md:155`, Start Approval not started), and nothing in this
repository can establish that the game will achieve it. **Proof weakness worth recording**: the
bet-level report is a dated artefact, not a gate. `.github/workflows/validate-math.yml` is wired
and enforces a different rubric, described in its own header at lines 3 to 7,

hit-rate / StdDev / max-win / wincap-odds from the SHIPPED lookup tables and enforces Stake
Engine's compliance rubric (RTP 90-98%, cross-mode <=0.5%, base hit-rate 5-33%, min 100k rows,
max-win reachable)". It does not check maximum exposure, maximum payout multiplier, CVaR, ETL or
P(>=5000x) per star tier, so if a lookup table were ever republished, the star-tier limits would
be unverified until someone re-ran `scripts/qa/bet_level_compliance.py` by hand. No self-test
question arises for the report since it is evidence rather than a gate, but the gate that does
exist has no seeded self-test step in either workflow that I could find.

this repository can defend any of them**. I confirmed the shape of the CI rather than assuming it:

code, locale, dist and maths-tooling gates, and a matrix browser job (line 668, `${{ matrix.gate.name }}`)

`WRS_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md`, `SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md`, the compliance register or the tracker. That is
not necessarily wrong, since a commercial deadline cannot be unit tested, but it does mean the only
available proof kinds for this surface are committed EVIDENCE and TRACKER_ROW, and of the seven
requirements exactly one has either. The cheapest structural fix is a dated owner-actions row set
in `docs/records/reviews/REVIEW_TRACKER.md` for the four requirements that carry a date or a
