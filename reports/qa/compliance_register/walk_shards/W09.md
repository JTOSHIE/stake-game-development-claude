## PER REQUIREMENT NOTES

**The shared frame, so each note below is not nine copies of it.** All nine rows are served by
one layer, opened in full: `frontend/src/lib/i18n/vocabulary.ts`. Its `PROHIBITED_TERMS` array
(`:44-84`) is the platform table transcribed verbatim including the platform's own duplicates,
`SUBSTITUTIONS` (`:95-105`) is derived from it rather than retyped ("longest first so `total bet`
wins over `bet`"), and `sv()` (`:156-164`) is the rewrite. Above that sit two keyed layers that do
the swap before `sv()` is ever needed: `SOCIAL_OVERRIDES` in `translations.ts:1965-2032` and
`PROSE_SOCIAL` in `prose.ts:184-212`.

Two CI gates are really wired, both in the `static` job (`checks.yml:104-106`, `name: static gates`):
gate 6a `a11y_social_terms_check.mjs` at `:233-235`, and gate 6b `vocabulary.test.ts` at `:246-248`.
I confirmed the wiring by reading the steps, not by the filenames.

Three weaknesses apply to ALL NINE rows and are the reason none of these should be read as
airtight:

1. **Neither gate has a convention (p) seeded self-test.** `grep -rln "SELF_TEST\|selfTest\|self-test\|--selftest" frontend/scripts` returns fifteen scripts and `a11y_social_terms_check.mjs`
   is not among them; `vocabulary.test.ts` has no seeded-violation mode either. `checks.yml` runs
   `--self-test` for the locked-paths, dash, machine-tell, asset-reference, books-verifier and six
   browser gates, and for neither social gate. So the two gates defending nine platform rows have
   never been seen to go red.
2. **The only full-surface instrument is not wired into CI.** `frontend/scripts/social_dom_conformance.mjs`
   walks the whole rendered DOM in both modes against all 39 rows (`:16-35`), and grep of
   `checks.yml` for `social_dom_conformance` returns nothing but a comment at `:245` citing its
   committed JSON. Its stated reason, `:42-43`, is "Playwright and a dev server, so it stays local
   and in the external audit per the CI workflow's stated scope". **That justification is now stale**:
   `checks.yml:561-590` is a `browser` job that runs seven Playwright gates with their self-tests.
   Grade for that script alone: SCRIPT_UNWIRED.
3. **The committed evidence predates the two changes that altered how social strings resolve.**
   `reports/qa/social_dom_conformance_2026-07-25.json:2` reads `"generated": "2026-07-25"`. The rules
   and interface guide moved out of the component and into the locale layer on 2026-07-28 (JOB 2,
   recorded at `vocabulary.test.ts:95-107`) and the win banner unit changed on 2026-07-29 (TR-117,
   `vocabulary.test.ts:137-150`). The 3242-string harvest therefore describes a resolution path that
   has since been rewritten twice and has not been regenerated.

**REQ-021, stake. This is the row where the mechanism deliberately does not apply, and it is worth
reading closely.** `vocabulary.ts:112-123` exempts it: `stake: 'brand and platform name; scanned,

"stake" that appears in a visible string, and gate 6b will never report one:
`vocabulary.test.ts:78-80` pins that exemption rather than the requirement, `check('excludes the
never-rewrite terms by default', scanProhibited('Stake Engine'), [])`. What does defend it: gate 6a
carries `'stake'` in its RESTRICTED list (`a11y_social_terms_check.mjs:40`) so an a11y literal would
fail CI; and the DOM evidence reports zero occurrences in the harvest
(`social_dom_conformance_2026-07-25.json:65`, `"socialInformationalHits": []`, populated at
`social_dom_conformance.mjs:281` from the never-rewrite set). I checked the exemption's factual claim
rather than trusting it: grep for "stake" across `translations.ts`, `prose.ts`, `prose.locales.ts`,
all components and `App.svelte` returns only code comments, and I opened each of the three that a
comment filter could have missed to confirm they are comments rather than markup:
`PaytableModal.svelte:406` `<!-- Disclaimer (Stake Engine seven-point requirement) -->`,
`HudOverlay.svelte:528-529` `<!-- MINI-PLAYER HUD (R2R-R JOB C / TR-043): a dedicated 44px single row for` /
`Stake's 400x225 popout. ...`, and `ReplayMode.svelte:343-344` inside a comment that ends `-->`.
Settled decisively against the shipped artefact rather than the source: `grep -oiE "stake[^ \"',;<>]{0,14}"`

`vocabulary.ts` table's own key and phrase, so no markup comment carrying "Stake's" reached `dist` and
no platform branding ships. So the requirement holds today. The exposure is that it
