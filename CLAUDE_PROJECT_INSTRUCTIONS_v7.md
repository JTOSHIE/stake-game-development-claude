3. CONVENTIONS (binding on all outputs)
(a) Australian English, metric, and no em dashes or en dashes anywhere in any output, including inside briefs and committed documents.
(b) Complete ready-to-use deliverables only. Anything Josh must copy is one fenced code block per item, finished, zero edits required; where a decision is Josh's, ship complete variants to choose between rather than fill-in blanks.
(c) Claude Code briefs are one code block pasted into the builder chat; the session saves and commits the brief verbatim; no terminal file transport; briefs are designed for unattended autonomous execution.
(d) Every Fable reply ends with one minimal paragraph of Josh's required actions plus full URLs.
(e) Phone-first: numbered steps for any new tool or surface.
(f) HARD LOCKS: frontend/src/lib/services/rgsService.ts, frontend/src/lib/stores/gameStore.ts, games/future_spinner/ (the entire maths package), and .claude/settings.json is never committed with changes. Owner-sanctioned exceptions are granted per pass by a brief naming the exact deny lines to lift; the lift is a temporary working-tree edit restored with a verified empty diff before any commit. Writing to locked paths via Bash is forbidden and never counts as an exception. Known hardcodes inside locked files that are compensated frontend-side are recorded in CLAUDE.md under LOCKED_FILE_DEBTS (currently: canBuyBonus 100x and canSpin 1x affordability checks, both compensated and unreachable via live UI) and ride along with any future sanctioned locked pass.
(g) Money maths: integer micros, Math.floor(dollars x 1_000_000); the 1,000,000 wallet scale and the 100 book scale are never mixed; all currency arithmetic flows through the micros helpers; a permanent cost-integrity gate in the QA soak asserts exact per-mode debits.
(h) Proofs: screenshots and renders that verdicts rely on are committed under reports/screens/<pass>/; QA evidence under reports/qa/ with dated filenames; superseded artefacts are explicitly marked or archived, never left ambient (a stale HANDOVER once cost a 2-star external audit misread).
(j) Living handover: one handover document per arc with dated appended sections; a fresh document only at a new arc.
(k) Commits stage explicit paths only, never git add -A or commit -a (a dirty-tree commit once leaked an unrelated package.json line onto main).
(l) Determinism and provenance: generation pipelines (AssetForge for art, AudioForge for sound, master.py for audio mastering) are seeded, logged and re-runnable per item; provenance logs and licence copies are committed as dossier evidence.
(m) DERIVE BEFORE MEASURING (standard operating procedure, ratified by the owner 2026-07-27). This project is surrounded by compliance, facts, figures and mathematics; there is no interpretation and no imagination. Derive the closed-form answer from the specification first (game_config.py, the PAR sheet, fsModes.ts, docs/stake-engine-live/) and cite the exact file and line. Measurement CONFIRMS a derived answer, it never discovers one; a measurement disagreeing with the specification is a broken measurement until proven otherwise. Every number carries a checkable source or it is reported as not known. Corroboration counts only when the two methods share no input, because shared inputs share their flaws. Self-audit before reporting, not after: re-derive, re-check against the specification, confirm locks and the stake-engine requirement as written. Unsolved beats wrongly solved: park with options rather than filling a gap with a plausible answer. Compliance text is quoted verbatim with its date, never paraphrased. Maths-adjacent findings escalate to the owner and Fable as questions with evidence, never ruled on by the builder. Full text and the worked example that produced it are in CLAUDE.md convention (l).

5. PLATFORM REALITY (mined first-hand from stake-engine.com approval docs; mirrored under docs/stake-engine-live/)
Review: three anonymous reviewers score on a fractional scale (0 to 3 in about 0.33 steps); the rounded average is the star tier; an average below 1.0 is not published, the thread locks for 7 days, then resubmission is allowed. Post-approval lockdown: cosmetic updates only, no math, mode or gameplay changes ever. Hard technical rules: the build is static and may reach NO external source (external fonts are the classic failure; Orbitron is self-hosted, and the conformance sweep asserts every request is same-origin); mini-player popout must render undistorted; spacebar maps to the bet button; every bet level from the authenticate response must be usable with minStep respected; per-mode cost, RTP and max win must be displayed to the player in-game along with all symbol pays, special values, feature access descriptions and a UI button guide; multi-win outcomes count up incrementally; sound can be disabled; autoplay needs explicit confirmation; language parameters must not corrupt text; social-mode strings must suit stake.us. The game tile is composed in the dashboard Tile Editor from uploaded layers (background render, transparent foreground hero, gradient, title), and the provider logo is a one-time square upload in Team Settings Branding. Team name, game title and assets must clear IP and trademark law: infringement is grounds for rejection. Released games start at the bottom of New Releases and re-rank every Friday, Australian time. Live enforcement numbers and their deltas are read from COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md and the newest dated capture under docs/stake-engine-live/, never from this paragraph.
