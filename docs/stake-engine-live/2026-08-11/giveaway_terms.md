What is the competition?

The Competition invites eligible game developers to build and submit a game on the Stake Engine Platform for the chance to win a prize. The Competition is a game of skill whereby chance plays no part in determining the winner. The Competition is open from 9am AEDT on 24 May 2026 closes on the Closing Date (Competition Period). Entries submitted after the Closing Date will not be considered. Stake Engine may extend or shorten the Competition Period by publishing an updated Closing Date on the Stake Engine website, effective immediately upon publication.

Are you eligible?

The Competition is open to individuals who, at the time of entry: (a) are 18 years of age or older (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, whichever is higher); (b) hold a valid, registered Stake Engine account; and (c) are not resident in, located in, or entering from any of the following jurisdictions: the United States of America; the Province of Quebec, Canada; Brazil; Cuba; Iran; North Korea; Syria; Russia; Belarus; or any other jurisdiction where participation in this Competition is prohibited by applicable law (Excluded Territories).

The following persons are not eligible: employees, officers, directors, contractors or agents of the Promoter or any related body corporate; their immediate family members; and any person legally prohibited from participating in trade promotions or skill competitions in their jurisdiction. Any entry submitted from an Excluded Territory is void. The Promoter may verify eligibility at any time and disqualify any entrant who is not eligible.

How do you enter?

To enter the Competition, an eligible applicant must before the Closing Date: (a) register for a Stake Engine account at https://stake-engine.com/signin (if you do not already hold one); (b) build and develop an original game using the Stake Engine Platform; and (c) submit your completed game via the Stake Engine Platform in accordance with Stake Engine's submission process and guidelines.

To be eligible for the Prize, your game must achieve a minimum 3-star rating (3 Star Rating) which will be determined at Stake Engine's sole discretion upon consideration of (but not limited to): game quality, originality, playability, technical execution and commercial potential.

Each entrant may submit multiple games and each game must independently achieve the 3 Star Rating to be considered.

Submitted games must: (a) be an original work created by you; (b) comply with Stake Engine's content and technical submission guidelines; (c) not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights; and (d) not contain unlawful, offensive or defamatory content or otherwise breach the Stake Engine Terms and Conditions. How can I be the Winner?

The winner of the Competition will be selected on a merit basis from all Qualifying Entries received by the Closing Date (Winner). There will only be one Winner.

The Promoter will assess entries against the following criteria, including but not limited to: game quality, originality, playability, technical execution and commercial potential. The judging panel will consist of four executives of Stake Engine, with the Promoter's selection being final.

The Winner will be notified by email from info@stake-engine.com within 14 Business Days of the Closing Date. If the Promoter cannot contact the Winner within 10 Business Days of notification (despite reasonable attempts), the Promoter may select an alternative winner and the original Winner forfeits the Prize. The Winner's name or username will be announced on https://x.com/StakeEngineRGS and https://discord.com/channels/1361168658457559070/1414734405997559858 on or around 19 August 2026. By entering, you consent to this announcement.
