## A. The two things flagged this session

### A.1 The bet cost. Our game is right; the platform's Bets panel is confusing.

The owner: *"For the bonus buys, it's showing us 200,000 or 400,000, but then it's only
showing us the bet price is 500 or 1,000."*

**Both numbers are correct, and they are measuring different things.** The platform's Bets
panel row shows **COST = the bet level**. The expanded detail shows **Cost (USD) = what was
actually debited**. For the 06:25:18 round the row reads `super $1,000.00` and the detail
reads `Cost (USD) $400,000.00`, `Cost multiplier x400.00`. Super costs 400x, so a $1,000 bet
level costs $400,000. That is what our maths specifies and what the platform charged.

**Our own confirm dialog shows the true price up front**, verified in two captures: at a $500
bet the NITRO OVERDRIVE dialog reads `PRICE $200,000.00`, and at $1,000 it reads
`PRICE $400,000.00`. So the game is not hiding the cost anywhere.

**The trap is the platform's MULT column, and it is worth understanding.** It is
payout divided by the **bet level**, not by what you paid:

| Mode | Bet | True cost | Payout | Platform MULT | Actual return on cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| super | $1,000 | $400,000 | $102,930 | **x102.93** | **0.257x** |
| super | $1,000 | $400,000 | $60,270 | x60.27 | 0.151x |
| super | $500 | $200,000 | $50,395 | x100.79 | 0.252x |
| super | $500 | $200,000 | $72,800 | x145.60 | 0.364x |
| super | $500 | $200,000 | $57,215 | x114.43 | 0.286x |
| bonus | $500 | $50,000 | $144,350 | x288.70 | **2.887x** |
| bonus | $500 | $50,000 | $21,125 | x42.25 | 0.422x |
| bonus | $500 | $50,000 | $44,760 | x89.52 | 0.895x |

A green `+$102,930.00` at `x102.93` looks like a large win. It was a **loss of $297,070**.
Across all eight buy rounds the owner staked $1,550,000 and got back $553,845, a 35.7%
return, which is a perfectly ordinary run given super's break-even rate of 71.8%.

**No fix on our side. Nothing to change.** Recorded so it is not re-investigated.

## B. Confirmed defects, ours to fix

### B.1 Buy-mode rounds present a loss as a win (NEW, TR-068)

**Priority: high, and it is the most player-facing thing on this list.**

The WIN panel shows the round's gross payout. For base play that is fine and conventional.
For buy modes it is actively misleading, because **the typical buy round pays back less than
it cost**: super's break-even rate is 71.8% and bonus's is 76.5%, so roughly three rounds in
four are "wins" that lose money. Seven of the owner's eight buy rounds were.

Showing gross payout is the industry convention and is not wrong in itself. The problem is
that at 400x it makes the common case read as the opposite of what happened, and the person
it confused was the owner, who knows the maths. It also sits directly against guideline item
50, *"UI clearly displays bet cost and applied multiplier"*.

**Options:**
- (a) Show the cost alongside the win in the buy-round result, for example
  `WIN $57,215 / COST $200,000`. Most honest, smallest change, no convention broken.
- (b) Show a net figure for buy rounds instead of gross. Clearest, but breaks the genre
  convention and would look wrong to a reviewer expecting a WIN readout.
- (c) Show the multiplier against cost (`x0.29`) rather than against bet level.
- (d) Leave it, on the grounds that it is standard.

**Recommend (a)**, with (c) as a cheap addition. Fable to rule, since it is player-money
presentation and therefore a (l.8) escalation rather than a builder call.

### B.2 The game frame scrolls on every responsive layout (TR-065, guideline 15)

`App.svelte:1821` gives `.game-wrapper.portrait`, `.compact-landscape` and `.mini-player`
`overflow-y: auto`. Scrollbars visible in captures at mobile portrait, Mobile S and Popout S,
absent on desktop, which is exactly that class list. **One CSS rule**, but check why `auto`
was chosen so the fix clips nothing.

### B.3 Double-tap zoom is not disabled (TR-066, guideline 25)

`frontend/index.html` has no `maximum-scale` or `user-scalable=no`, and `touch-action`
appears nowhere in `frontend/src`. Note the meta tag alone is ignored by modern iOS Safari,
so the durable fix is `touch-action: manipulation` on interactive surfaces. **Small.**

### B.4 The mini-player WIN readout clips mid-glyph (TR-066)

At Popout S the strip renders `$0.0` plus a cut character, and the balance drops its cents
(`BAL $1,040` where the true balance is $1,040.06). The dropped cents may be intentional at
400px; the clipping is not. **Small, but it is in the sizing that has already been tuned
twice, so measure rather than nudge.**

### B.5 Social Mode never forces English (TR-067, guideline 46)

`i18n/tr.ts:14` derives from `[locale, isSocial]` but social selects only the vocabulary
variant, never the locale, so a social session with `lang=de` renders German. **One line**,
pending a ruling on whether to force or to treat the guideline as a statement about platform
behaviour.

### B.6 The published bundle is a commit behind (TR-062)

The V2 re-upload sent four images and did not rebuild, so the em dashes TR-060 removed are
still shipping and still visible in the owner's paytable capture. **The fix is a fresh-clone
rebuild**, which should carry B.2 through B.5 in the same bundle. Also: nothing in `dist/`
records which commit built it, which is why this needed grepping to establish.

### B.7 Player-facing prose is not localised (TR-059)

Roughly 30 keys times 16 locales plus the `fsModes.ts` strings. **A pass of its own**, not a
patch. Now cheaper than it was, because the DTT's Local Testing redirect URL lets it be
tested against real RGS from a local dev server.

### B.8 The dash gate cannot see the string form it was built for (TR-063)

It reads single-quoted JavaScript literals only, and the strings it was written to catch were
markup prose. It also does not scan `App.svelte`, which ships an em dash in the document
title. **Recommend moving the gate onto `dist/`** so it checks what actually ships, which
would also have caught B.6.

## C. Escalated, must not be fixed blind

### C.1 Zero-win end-round: the client and the checklist contradict each other (TR-064)

Guideline item 12 says zero-win bets must not send an end-round request. Our gate is the
round's `active` flag, changed deliberately under the JOB 4 sanction because the official
client says *"Only call this API if Play() has returned an Active result"*. **Reverting to a
win-based gate would reintroduce the defect that sanction closed.** One Network-tab capture
on a zero-win spin settles it. **Highest-value single observation left in the project.**
