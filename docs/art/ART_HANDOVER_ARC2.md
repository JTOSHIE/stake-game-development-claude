# ART HANDOVER, ARC 2

**Purpose.** Future Spinner was submitted to Stake Engine and scored **4.3 of 9** against a
6-point bar. It was not published. The three tags returned were **low quality assets, poor
animations, bad sound design**. No compliance, functional or correctness issue was raised
anywhere in the feedback. This document is the visual half of the answer: what ships today,
what each file does, what must be honoured by anything replacing it, and what must NOT be
replaced.

Read this beside `docs/art/art_manifest_arc2.csv`, which is the machine-readable version,
one row per file, and the contact sheets at `reports/art/arc2/current/`, which show the
starting state grouped by priority.

**Everything here was measured at the tip, not remembered.** Dimensions, alpha and byte
sizes are read from each file's own header. Render sites are read from the components that
draw them. Where a claim could not be verified it is not made.

## 1. THE ONE ASSET THAT MUST NOT CHANGE

**`ui/hero_emblem_512.png`, the We Roll Spinners hero emblem, is the sole KEEP** by owner
ruling. It is not up for replacement, restyling or recolouring, and every new asset in the
game must harmonise with it rather than the other way round.

- Shipped at **512x512, palette PNG, NO alpha channel**, 120.7 KB. It is an opaque square.
  Anything designed to sit beside it should not assume a cut-out silhouette.
- Derived from `design-system/brand/hero_emblem/master_1024.png`, 1024x1024 RGB, opaque.
- **It carries a Google Gemini SynthID watermark**, and its licence capture is archived at
  `docs/licences/google-gemini/2026-07-15/`. Its provenance record is
  `design-system/brand/hero_emblem/GENERATION_NOTE.md`.

**Its colour story, which is the anchor for the whole arc**, quoted from the generation
prompt in that record: a "neon palette (magenta, purple, cyan over dark gunmetal)", a
"circular chrome car wheel with dark tyre and neon tread glow", an "embedded three-reel slot
cylinder showing neon 7 7 7", arched text WE ROLL over SPINNERS, on "a single flat, very dark
plain colour (near-black, #0a0a12)".

`ui/hero_icon_96.png` is derived from the same master but **renders nowhere**, see section 4.

## 2. WHAT SHIPS, AND THE NUMBER THE BRIEF ESTIMATED

The brief estimated "~115 under themes/future-spinner, assets/ui and symbols". **That is
exactly the SOURCE count** in those three roots: 47 + 23 + 45 = 115. **Only 47 rasters
actually ship.**

`assets/ui/` and `assets/symbols/` are pre-LAYOUT_SPEC legacy roots, fully pruned by the
build and dead since WinPod was deleted on 2026-08-13. **Sixty-eight of the 115 never reach a
player, and none of them should be sent to a generation tool.** The manifest covers the 47
that ship.

| Disposition | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **KEEP** | 1 | The hero emblem. Do not touch. |
| **REPLACE** | 30 | Genuine art, open for replacement. |
| **REGEN** | 10 | Documentation icons. Do NOT hand these to an artist, see section 3. |
| **DEAD** | 6 | Ships but renders nowhere. Delete, do not redraw, see section 4. |

**So the real art brief is 30 files, not 115.**

## 3. TEN FILES THAT LOOK LIKE UI ART AND ARE NOT

`spin_button`, `btn_bet_plus`, `btn_bet_minus`, `btn_max`, `btn_autoplay`, `btn_menu`, the
three `btn_turbo` files and `btn_features` are **documentation icons**. They appear only
inside the paytable's Interface Guide.

> **AMENDED R125 (2026-08-26).** This list used to end `feature_button`, and the section
> heading still says TEN because the count is unchanged - but the tenth file is now
> `btn_features.png`, not `feature_button.png`. The guide's Features row was the ONE row
> pointing at a painted manifest badge instead of a capture of the live control, which is
> exactly the drift this section warns about, present in the section's own list. It now
> points at `btn_features.png`, a capture of the live `.fm-entry-pill`. `feature_button.png`
> is no longer a documentation icon at all; see the dual-role note below, also amended. **The live controls they depict are drawn in CSS and
inline SVG**, not from these bitmaps; `HudOverlay.svelte:1708` says so outright, "Replaces
spin_button.png".

They are produced by **headless screenshot of the running app**,
`frontend/scripts/regen_interface_guide_icons.mjs`. The correct way to change them is to
restyle the live control and re-run that script. **A hand-drawn replacement would
immediately drift from the button it documents**, which is the exact failure
`interface_guide_icon_proof.mjs` exists to catch.

**AMENDED R125 (2026-08-26): `feature_button.png` is no longer dual-role, and the claim
below was wrong even before that.** It used to read: "the one dual-role file: it is
regenerated by that script AND renders live as the buy dialog header art, so a hand
replacement would be overwritten on the next regen."

Two corrections. First, it was never regenerated by `regen_interface_guide_icons.mjs` -
that script's `TARGETS` has never contained it; it comes from `build.py` via
`manifest.json`. That error was already recorded once (the DOC-10 note claiming "ten
targets" when there were nine) and is corrected here at its source. Second, since R125 the
Interface Guide points at `btn_features.png` instead, so `feature_button.png` now has
exactly ONE role: the buy dialog header art at `BuyBonus.svelte:117`. It is a normal
manifest-built raster, safe to replace by hand through the manifest, and nothing regenerates
over it.

**The turbo mapping, confirmed from the regenerator's own click sequence:** `btn_turbo` =
NORMAL, `btn_turbo_2` = TURBO, `btn_turbo_3` = SUPER TURBO. Speed is encoded by **intensity
alone**, no numeral and no caption. Any restyle must keep the three monotonic and separable
at a glance, must add no animation (a pulse passes Turbo through Super Turbo brightness and
the two stop being distinguishable), and must keep the three files byte-distinct.

## 4. SIX FILES THAT SHIP AND RENDER NOWHERE

Delete these; **do not pay to have them redrawn.**

| File | Why it is dead |
|---|---|
| `frames/frame-1.png` | Superseded by frame-2. `themeStore.ts:75` hardcodes frame-2 for every theme. |
| `ui/subtitle.png` | Zero references in source and absent from the built bundle. |
| `ui/panel_balance.png` | `themeStore.ts:85` derives the path; no component reads it. |
| `ui/panel_win.png` | `themeStore.ts:86` derives the path; no component reads it. |
| `ui/scene_character_car.png` | Retired baked composite, superseded by the two separate sprites. |
| `ui/hero_icon_96.png` | Its only reader was LoadingScreen, removed by owner ruling 2026-08-09. |

**Two of these need more than a delete.**

`ui/scene_character_car.png` **regenerates itself**: `scripts/assets/manifest.json:26` still
exports it and `build.py` renders that block unconditionally, so deleting the PNG alone
restores it on the next `npm run assets`. **The same stale-export class covers two files
that were already deleted**, ui/brand_mark.png and ui/brand_mark_glyph.png at
`scripts/assets/manifest.json:22-23`: running the asset build today recreates both and takes
the theme folder from 47 rasters to 50. Fix all three export entries in one edit.

`ui/hero_icon_96.png` is derived from the PROTECTED emblem master, so it must be deleted
alongside LoadingScreen rather than regenerated by an art tool.

**And a lesson for the replacements**, from the two dead panels: both **bake English words**
into the art, BALANCE and WIN. That cannot survive sixteen locales, and social mode remaps
WIN to PRIZE. `panel_balance` also carries garbled machine-generated pseudo-text in its
readout window. **No replacement may bake copy into a raster.**

## 5. A LIVE VISUAL DEFECT THE REPLACEMENT MUST FIX

**The Overdrive meter renders two needles.**

`ui/gauge_face.png` is a whole render of `H2_master_v31.svg`, and that master contains the
needle group at `rotate(62 512 512)`, so **the dial has a needle baked in at 62 degrees**.
The live `ui/gauge_needle.png` sprite then rotates on top of it
(`BonusInstrumentColumn.svelte:85` and `:88`). Both are confirmed by reading the component
and by looking at the shipped files: the baked needle is plainly visible on the P3 contact
sheet.

The pipeline already knew: `scripts/assets/manifest.json:38` declares a needle-free
a needle-free gauge_base as the intended under-layer, with a note reading "Engine rotates
gauge_needle over gauge_base". Nothing ever referenced it, so it was correctly deleted as
unreferenced in 2026-08-09 under TR-112, and the bug survived.

**The replacement dial MUST be needle-free.** And `gauge_needle.png` is drawn **pre-rotated
to +62 degrees**, so every angle in the code is 62 degrees off what the art shows: supply the
replacement at the same baked 62 degrees, or supply it at 0 and correct the call site in the
same change. Live sweep is -110 to +110 degrees for 1x to 16x.

## 6. HARD CONSTRAINTS, PER ASSET CLASS

**Animation sheets. The frame layout is not negotiable**, all single-row, left to right, no
padding and no margin between frames:

| Sheet | Frames | Frame size | Canvas |
|---|---|---|---|
| `symbols/m3_flame_sheet.png` | 6 | 200x200 | 1200x200 |
| `symbols/l2_fuse_sheet.png` | 4 | 200x200 | 800x200 |
| `ui/jet_flame_sheet.png` | 5 | 240x120 | 1200x120 |

`ui/jet_flame_static.png` is **literally frame 3 of the jet sheet**, verified pixel-identical.
Export it from the replacement sheet rather than drawing it separately.

**Symbols are 240x240 transparent PNG** and sit inside a cell inset of about 82 per cent, so
the art needs margin. Three carry idle animations that need headroom: `l3` pumps 7px, `m2`
bobs 3px, `l1` wants a facet for its glint sweep.

**`symbols/scatter.png` rotates a full 360 degrees over 12 seconds.** The replacement must be
radially symmetric or designed to spin, or it will visibly tumble.

**`symbols/h1` is three files, not one.** The reels composite `h1_base` plus `h1_spin`, split
by symbol ID and never by game state, and `h1.png` is the composed reference used only in the
paytable and buy preview. Supply all three together, aligned to the pixel, and make `h1_spin`
rotationally balanced about the exact image centre.

**`symbols/tile_plate.png` sits behind every symbol on the grid**, unconditionally, in all 5
columns and 7 strip slots. It is the highest-leverage single tile in the set: changing it
changes the entire reel field.

**Plate signature colours are applied by the engine as a CSS glow and are NOT baked into the
art** (`plates.json`). Do not bake a glow that will double with the engine's.

**`frames/frame-2.png` is squashed non-uniformly today**: an 800x640 source drawn into a
640x468 box with `object-fit: fill`, 0.800 in x against 0.731 in y. Author the replacement at
the true 640x468 aspect, or change the call site.

**Backgrounds are 1920x1080 opaque JPEG** painted at `object-fit: cover`, so a replacement
must survive a cover crop at both wide and tall aspects and stay dark enough under the HUD
strip for white text to hold contrast. `bg_overdrive` is a **colour grade of `bg_base` at
identical geometry**, not a separate scene: supply them as a matched pair or the 0.6s
crossfade will jump.

### Type

Both faces are **self-hosted via @fontsource**; the build may reach no external source, and
an external font request is the classic submission failure.

| Role | Face | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Brand and headings | **Orbitron** | `--fs-font-display` |
| Money and counting | **Exo 2** | `--fs-font-numeric` |

**One measured caveat if the numeric face changes:** Orbitron is NOT tabular, with a digit
advance spread of 44.30 at 100px and no `tnum` feature, which is why the counting face is Exo
2. A replacement numeric face must be tabular or the counters will wobble as they roll.

### Licence requirement

**Every generated or purchased replacement must be commercially usable in real-money
gambling, and that must be verified BEFORE it ships, not after.** The platform's own rule is
that team name, game title and assets must clear IP and trademark law, and infringement is
grounds for rejection.

In practice, for each replacement: record the tool or vendor, the licence terms as fetched on
the day, and whether the terms permit commercial use in a gambling context specifically.
Several generative tools permit commercial use generally while restricting gambling, so the
general permission is not enough. The existing pattern to copy is the emblem's own record,
`design-system/brand/hero_emblem/GENERATION_NOTE.md`, with the terms archived in-repo at
`docs/licences/`. Assets whose licence cannot be established do not ship.

**Note also that AI provenance is now a scoring risk and not only a licence one.** The live
quality-rankings page names "over-reliance on generic AI-generated assets, standard fonts,
gradients, emoji icons, and border effects" as a frequent cause of a 1-star rating. That
sentence should be read as feedback on the set this document inventories.
