# Books manifest

**The public fingerprint of a deliberately private set.**

The five event books are **not in this repository and never will be**. This file is the
bridge: it records exactly what exists, so anyone reading the repository can verify the
private set without possessing it.

Generated 2026-07-28. Australian English, no em dashes or en dashes.

## Why the books are not committed (Fable ruling, 2026-07-28)

Three reasons, on the record:

1. **Bandwidth.** Git LFS on a public repository burns quota on every clone, including
   the daily verification clones Fable performs.
2. **No submission requirement.** The platform receives books by **ACP upload**, not via
   GitHub. Committing them satisfies nothing the platform asks for.
3. **Decisive: the books ARE the game.** They are the complete outcome distribution of
   all five modes. Publishing them to a public repository pre-release hands the entire
   product, every result and its weighting, to anyone who clones it.

A fourth, purely mechanical point makes the alternative impossible anyway:
`books_bonus.jsonl.zst` (144.9 MB) and `books_super.jsonl.zst` (142.4 MB) both exceed
**GitHub's hard 100 MB per-file limit**, so a plain commit could not succeed.

## Cross-checks performed

- **Row count matches the lookup tables.** Every book holds 100,000 rows; every
  `lookUpTable_<mode>_0.csv` holds 100,000 rows. One row per simulation, five modes.
- **Mode set matches `index.json`.** The five declared modes (`base`, `cruise`,
  `antelite`, `bonus`, `super`) each have exactly one book and one lookup table, and the
  filenames match the `events` and `weights` fields the index declares.
- **Hashes match the dossier.** All five SHA-256 values are byte-identical to those
  recorded in `SUBMISSION_DOSSIER.md` section 5c. The dossier's hash record was always
  correct; its only defect was implying the files sit in the repository, which section
  5c now states explicitly.

## Provenance

Generated by `games/future_spinner/run.py` from the frozen, owner-sanctioned simulation
pass. Per `CLAUDE.md`, the raw simulation stage is deterministic and seeded, and
published lookup tables are frozen truth: never regenerated outside a sanctioned pass,
and even then only to fill a genuinely missing file, never to refresh one that is
already correct. `books_super.jsonl.zst` was the one regeneration, on 2026-07-14, under
a temporary scoped lift of the `games/future_spinner/**` deny lines, restored with a
verified-empty diff.

## What this manifest does NOT yet prove

**Row-by-row book-to-lookup payout equality is not proven here.** That is R3's remaining
work: decode every row of every book and prove the summed event payout equals the
lookup payout, for all 100,000 rows in all five modes. Review 1 named this as the thing
it could not verify, and it is the reason the manifest exists rather than a bare
assertion.

Until that runs, this manifest establishes **identity and integrity** (the right files,
unmodified, with the right shape), not **semantic equivalence**.
