(m) DERIVE BEFORE MEASURING (standard operating procedure, ratified by the owner 2026-07-27). This project is surrounded by compliance, facts, figures and mathematics; there is no interpretation and no imagination. Derive the closed-form answer from the specification first (game_config.py, the PAR sheet, fsModes.ts, docs/stake-engine-live/) and cite the exact file and line. Measurement CONFIRMS a derived answer, it never discovers one; a measurement disagreeing with the specification is a broken measurement until proven otherwise. Every number carries a checkable source or it is reported as not known. Corroboration counts only when the two methods share no input, because shared inputs share their flaws. Self-audit before reporting, not after: re-derive, re-check against the specification, confirm locks and the stake-engine requirement as written. Unsolved beats wrongly solved: park with options rather than filling a gap with a plausible answer. Compliance text is quoted verbatim with its date, never paraphrased. Maths-adjacent findings escalate to the owner and Fable as questions with evidence, never ruled on by the builder. Full text and the worked example that produced it are in CLAUDE.md convention (l).
(p) Every gate that claims a class closed ships a self-test that plants the exact defect in the form it really occurs and must be seen to go RED before its PASS counts. A gate that has never been seen to fail is not evidence, it is a script that prints PASS. This binds existing gates as much as new ones, and it binds them AFTER the world changes: a width-tuned seed can be silently disarmed by a font, a layout or a copy change while the gate's own assertions stay green.

5. PLATFORM REALITY (mined first-hand from stake-engine.com approval docs; mirrored under docs/stake-engine-live/)
Review: three anonymous reviewers score on a fractional scale (0 to 3 in about 0.33 steps); the rounded average is the star tier; an average below 1.0 is not published, the thread locks for 7 days, then resubmission is allowed. Post-approval lockdown: cosmetic updates only, no math, mode or gameplay changes ever. Hard technical rules: the build is static and may reach NO external source (external fonts are the classic failure; BOTH shipped faces are self-hosted via @fontsource, Orbitron as the brand and heading face and Exo 2 as the money and counting face, and the conformance sweep asserts every request is same-origin); mini-player popout must render undistorted; spacebar maps to the bet button; every bet level from the authenticate response must be usable with minStep respected; per-mode cost, RTP and max win must be displayed to the player in-game along with all symbol pays, special values, feature access descriptions and a UI button guide; multi-win outcomes count up incrementally; sound can be disabled; autoplay needs explicit confirmation; language parameters must not corrupt text; social-mode strings must suit stake.us. The game tile is composed in the dashboard Tile Editor from uploaded layers (background render, transparent foreground hero, gradient, title), and the provider logo is a one-time square upload in Team Settings Branding. Team name, game title and assets must clear IP and trademark law: infringement is grounds for rejection. Released games start at the bottom of New Releases and re-rank every Friday, Australian time. Live enforcement numbers and their deltas are read from COMPLIANCE_WATCH.md and the newest dated capture under docs/stake-engine-live/, never from this paragraph.
